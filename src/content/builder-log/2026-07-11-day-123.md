<!-- en -->

- I retired an investment signal feedback loop after its low hit rate and broken consumption path made further tuning feel like fake precision.
- I kept the scripts and historical evidence intact, so stopping the system does not erase what it taught us.
- I reframed the KOL scanner as a credibility ledger rather than a trade generator; observation and execution should not share the same threshold.
- I standardized the scheduled-agent runtime so recurring work now runs on one consistent foundation.
- I added fresh research angles across machine vision, satellite connectivity, and power semiconductors to expose portfolio blind spots.
- I kept weekend portfolio monitoring action-free: static prices and weekend narratives are context, not confirmation.
- I caught myself turning a simple copy request into a production plan; the better output was shorter, direct, and ready to use.

<!-- zh -->

- 我停掉了一条投资信号反馈闭环：命中率低、消费路径也断了，继续调参只是在制造假精度。
- 脚本和历史证据都保留下来，停止系统不等于抹掉它留下的教训。
- KOL 扫描被重新定义为信用档案，而不是交易生成器；观察和执行不该共用同一道门槛。
- 定时 Agent 的运行底座完成统一，重复任务终于回到一致的基础上。
- 研究侧补了机器视觉、卫星连接和功率半导体三个新角度，用来发现组合盲区。
- 周末组合监控保持零动作：静态价格和周末叙事只能提供背景，不能冒充成交确认。
- 我也抓到自己把一句简单文案扩成制作方案的惯性；真正更好的交付，是更短、更直接、拿来就能用。
