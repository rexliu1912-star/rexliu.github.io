<!-- en -->
- Connected a focused quote-post shortlist to the daily writing page, with room for an original angle and separate tracking from posts about my own articles.
- Gave recurring reports a shared reading order: the decision first, actions next, evidence below, and charts where comparisons need more than a paragraph.
- Borrowed editing and media-checking methods from two public bot examples; I kept the existing video formats instead of treating a useful idea as a reason to add another bot.
- Defined warm-paper illustrations for narrative work, kept data cards on their own visual track, and updated an existing essay cover to match.
- Added a change gate to task reconciliation, so an unchanged list does not need another round of device reads and rewritten handoffs.
- Turned a full video transcript into a bright English information card, keeping the speaker's claims attributed and the closing cautions intact.
- Revised and published the daily video companion after a real edit, while keeping separate original drafts marked as drafts rather than counting them as finished posts.

<!-- zh -->
- 把聚焦的外站引用帖短名单接进每日写作页，留出自己的切入角度，并与自有文章的配套帖分开记录。
- 给例行报告定了同一阅读顺序：先结论、再行动、后证据；需要比较的数据用图，不靠一大段文字硬讲。
- 从两个公开机器人案例里借了编辑和媒体核验方法；我保留原有视频模板，没有因为一个好点子就再养一个机器人。
- 叙事配图统一走暖纸插画，数据卡保留自己的视觉路线，也给一篇现有文章换上了对应封面。
- 给任务对账加了变化门：清单没变，就不必再读一轮设备、重写一遍交接。
- 把一段完整视频转录整理成明亮的英文信息卡，讲者自述保留归属，片尾的提醒也没有被摘要删掉。
- 按真实改稿重写并发布每日视频配文；另外两篇原创仍按草稿记，没有把排进计划当成已经发出。
