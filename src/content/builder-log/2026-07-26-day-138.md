<!-- en -->

- Reconciled reopened investment records as new lifecycle instances instead of quietly inheriting rules from earlier positions.
- Added a market-data gate that checks freshness, live-price deviation, displayed ranges, and source links before a report can ship.
- Made event date part of the journal storage key so weekly rollups cannot be distorted by entries appended to an older day.
- Broke AI infrastructure spending into compute, custom silicon, networking, storage, and power rather than treating total capex as one winner's revenue.
- Reviewed the week's content performance and separated a strong recurring series from the weaker health of the overall publishing cadence.
- Kept a high-density numbered recap as a useful format without promoting one good sample into a universal writing rule.
- Audited the next-week list and exposed how quickly a focus system becomes a backlog when nearly every item is carried forward.
- My clearest takeaway today: a field can be individually valid and still form a false state when its time, object identity, or destination is wrong.

<!-- zh -->

- 把重新建立的投资记录视为新的生命周期实例，不再悄悄继承早期仓位留下的旧规则。
- 给行情数据加上机器门禁：交付前检查新鲜度、实时价偏差、展示区间和来源链接。
- 把事件日期变成日志存储键的一部分，避免内容追加到旧日期后扭曲周度统计。
- 把 AI 基建投入拆成算力、定制芯片、网络、存储和电力，不再把 CapEx 总量直接等同于某一家公司的收入。
- 复盘本周内容表现时，把一个固定系列的强势表现和账号整体供给健康度分开判断。
- 高事件密度的编号周报值得保留，但不会因为一个好样本就被抬成普遍写作规则。
- 审计下周清单后再次确认：当几乎所有任务都在滚动，所谓焦点很快就会退化成 backlog。
- 我今天最清楚的收获是：字段各自都合法，时间、对象身份或目标桶错了，组合起来仍然会是假状态。
