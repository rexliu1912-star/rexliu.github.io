<!-- en -->
- I learned that a report can contain every required section and still fail if the supporting material takes more space than the actual discovery.
- The blind-spot review now gives new value-chain gaps and research cards the main stage, while historical tracking is compressed into one verification table.
- Each new research candidate must show where it sits in an existing value chain, what near-term evidence matters, and what would falsify the idea.
- KOL credibility is now rebuilt from the original claim, its reasoning chain, and real short- and long-window price outcomes instead of inherited scorekeeping.
- The weekly portfolio review kept a defensive posture: missing risk boundaries must be written before fresh exposure is discussed.
- The content review exposed a familiar trap—steady distribution can hide a weak pipeline of original ideas—so creation cadence is the next constraint to fix.
- My operating note for future reports is simple: completeness prevents omissions; emphasis determines whether the output is useful.

<!-- zh -->
- 我今天确认了一件事：报告可以每个板块都齐全，却依然失败——只要辅助材料比真正的新发现占得更多。
- 盲区周报现在让新价值链缺口和研究卡站到主位，历史追踪则压缩成一张负责验证的表。
- 每个新候选都必须说清它在现有价值链里的位置、近期该看什么证据，以及什么情况会证伪。
- KOL 信用不再继承旧评分，而是回到原始观点、推演逻辑和真实价格的短长窗口结果重新建立。
- 周度组合复盘继续保持防御：风险边界没有写清之前，不讨论新增暴露。
- 内容复盘也暴露了一个熟悉的陷阱：稳定分发会掩盖原创供给不足，下一步该修的是创作节奏。
- 留给以后报告的一句话是：完整性负责防漏，详略负责决定它到底有没有用。
