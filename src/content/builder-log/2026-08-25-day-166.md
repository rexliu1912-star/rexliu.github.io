<!-- en -->

- Rebuilt the morning portfolio section around cross-market daily change, cost context, and contribution attribution instead of another list of quotes.
- Kept price anomalies and material holding news as separate blocks, so a sharp move is not automatically treated as a thesis-changing event.
- Cut the KOL scanner from nine sources to four using mature, deduplicated accuracy samples, then capped the daily output at three cards.
- Closed the latest Nike earnings review without forcing a turnaround narrative: normalized margin improved, but Direct, China, and inventory still need proof.
- Distilled three podcast episodes into searchable canonical notes spanning semiconductor valuation, supply-chain resilience, and science-policy competition.
- The strongest idea today came from the Bronze Age collapse: resilience is less about avoiding networks than reducing irreplaceable inputs and single points of failure.
- I realized that “more complete” is often the wrong product goal; the useful system is the one that makes contribution, risk, and discipline visible in a few seconds.

<!-- zh -->

- 早报持仓段不再重复列报价，而是直接展示跨市场每日变化、成本位置和贡献归因。
- 把价格异动与重大持仓新闻拆成两块，避免一次大涨跌被自动解释成 thesis 变化。
- 用成熟且去重的准确率样本，把 KOL 扫描从 9 个来源收敛到 4 个，并把每日输出压到最多 3 张卡。
- 完成 Nike 最新财报复核，但没有硬讲反转故事：正常化毛利率在改善，Direct、中国和库存仍要继续验证。
- 将 3 集播客沉淀成可搜索的 canonical 笔记，覆盖芯片估值、供应链韧性和科研政策竞争。
- 今天最有意思的启发来自青铜时代崩塌：韧性不是逃离网络，而是减少不可替代投入和单点故障。
- 我意识到，“更完整”经常是错误的产品目标；真正有用的系统，是几秒内就让贡献、风险和纪律变得可见。
