<!-- en -->

- Restored a headless remote machine after tracing the outage to an expired network login, then verified both devices were visible again.
- Recorded a new index-fund trade in the portfolio source of truth and read the resulting position back instead of trusting a write response.
- Repaired four incomplete active-position cards so their theses, drivers, and sector context now render consistently.
- Cleaned the bilingual metadata for closed positions, removing the awkward gaps between English and Chinese views.
- Kept the portfolio in defense mode after the risk review showed that semiconductor exposure still needs discipline before another add.
- Traced a “missed” video to a simple clock mismatch: it was published after the daily discovery and transcription window.
- Decided not to chase every source in real time; commentary can arrive tomorrow, while earnings, portfolio news, and market voices carry the fast lane.
- I caught the larger trap tonight: maintenance is valuable when it removes risk, but once the system is safe, more polishing can become a respectable form of avoidance.
- Curated a small early-English reading stack that starts with shared picture books before turning learning into another dashboard.

- 远程机器断联后，定位到网络登录失效，完成无屏认证并确认两端重新可见。
- 把一笔新的指数基金交易写回组合事实源，并用最终持仓读回验收，而不是只信写入成功。
- 补齐四张 active position 卡片，让投资逻辑、驱动因素和赛道信息重新完整显示。
- 清理已清仓页面的双语元数据，消除中英文视图之间的空白和错位。
- 风险复盘后继续保持防御：半导体敞口先执行纪律，再谈下一次加仓。
- 查清一条“漏掉”的视频只是发布时间晚于当天发现与转录窗口，不是链路失效。
- 不再要求所有信息源都实时：评论内容可以次日看，财报、持仓新闻和市场观点才走快车道。
- 我今晚抓到一个更大的陷阱：维护在消除风险时有价值，但系统恢复安全后，继续打磨也可能只是体面的逃避。
- 整理了一套轻量英语启蒙读物，从亲子共读开始，不急着把学习再做成一张仪表盘。
