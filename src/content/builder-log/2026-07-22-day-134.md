<!-- en -->

- Fixed a monitoring bug where rules from a closed position survived after the same asset was opened again.
- Rebuilt risk cards from the current position state and made profit alerts respect the active cost basis.
- Removed stale closed-position artifacts and aligned the public display with the current source of truth.
- Turned a major AI security incident into a dense English visual explainer and a ready-to-share post.
- Converted a long-form essay about work, judgment, and the AI era into clean, searchable Markdown.
- Reworked an adaptive learning pipeline so daily and weekly plans must read a live capability profile before generating tasks.
- I noticed the same failure pattern in two very different systems: the object changed, but its derived rules did not.
- The new design rule is simple: every meaningful state transition must invalidate and rebuild downstream assumptions.

<!-- zh -->

- 修掉一个监控漏洞：仓位清掉后重新建立，同一标的的旧规则却还活着。
- 按当前仓位状态重建风险卡，并让止盈提醒真正尊重当前成本口径。
- 清理已失效的旧仓位残留，让公开展示重新对齐当前事实源。
- 把一场重要的 AI 安全事件做成高密度英文视觉卡片和可直接转发的文案。
- 将一篇讨论做事、判断与 AI 时代的长文整理成干净、可检索的 Markdown。
- 重构一条自适应学习管线：每日与每周计划生成前，必须先读取实时能力档案。
- 我发现两个完全不同的系统犯了同一种错：对象已经变了，派生规则却没有跟着变。
- 新原则很简单：每次关键状态跃迁，都要让下游旧假设失效并重建。
