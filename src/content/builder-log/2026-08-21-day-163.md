<!-- en -->

- Fixed an investment monitor that could keep a closed item alive because a local strategy table had drifted away from the portfolio source of truth.
- Added a hard reconciliation gate so active-trade views can only include positions that are actually active in the canonical portfolio.
- Audited a relative-strength thesis across short and medium horizons instead of accepting the cleanest-looking chart window.
- I deliberately changed the conclusion from “stronger overall” to “short-term rebound, reversal unconfirmed” when the longer window contradicted the original story.
- Turned the correction into a pullback re-entry checklist covering relative performance, drawdown, price structure, volume, moving averages, liquidity, and invalidation.
- Distilled a market podcast into three reusable lenses: real yields for gold, position sizing before averaging down, and customer traffic as the test for consumer turnarounds.
- Identified the next reliability improvement: closing or reopening a position should invalidate every batch-bound target, rule, and cache—not just hide stale output at render time.

<!-- zh -->

- 修掉一个投资监控里的过期状态：本地策略表与组合事实源分叉后，已清仓对象仍可能继续出现在活跃交易里。
- 增加事实源硬校验，活跃交易视图只允许展示组合主数据中真实存在的仓位。
- 没有只看最顺眼的图表窗口，而是同时检查短周期和中周期，重新审视一条相对强势判断。
- 当更长周期的数据与原叙事冲突时，我主动把结论从“整体更强”改成“短期反弹，反转未确认”。
- 把这次纠偏固化成回调重入清单：相对表现、回撤、价格结构、量能、均线、流动性与失效条件缺一不可。
- 完成一期市场播客的知识提炼，留下三个可复用视角：黄金看实际利率、补仓前先定首仓纪律、消费反转要看真实客流。
- 找到下一步可靠性改造：仓位清仓或重开时，所有绑定旧批次的目标、规则与缓存都应主动失效，而不是只在展示层遮住旧结果。
