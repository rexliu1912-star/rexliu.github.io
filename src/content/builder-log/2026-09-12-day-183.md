<!-- en -->

- Recalibrated the research loop from mature follow-up outcomes; a good first week no longer counts as proof of a durable trend.
- Added a recovery gate for deeply fallen candidates: research can continue, but action waits for sustained trend and volume confirmation.
- Mapped the robotics stack beyond perception and compute into motion control, reliability, and physical execution.
- Used sporting-goods channels as an evidence layer for a footwear turnaround, separating brand claims from sell-through, promotion, and inventory reality.
- Extended digital-asset research beyond token prices into regulated custody, transfer agency, settlement, and whether those services produce real fees.
- Closed the daily brokerage reconciliation with no unexpected activity and a consistent portfolio state.
- I liked the restraint in today's result: the system found worthwhile questions, but none deserved to become an actionable idea yet.

<!-- zh -->

- 用成熟的后续表现重新校准研究闭环：第一周上涨不再被当成趋势可以持续的证明。
- 为深度回撤候选增加恢复门禁：研究可以继续，但行动必须等趋势和成交量持续确认。
- 把机器人研究从感知与计算继续往下拆到运动控制、可靠性和物理执行层。
- 用运动零售渠道验证鞋服品牌反转，把品牌自述与真实动销、促销和库存分开看。
- 数字资产研究不再只看代币价格，而是继续追到受监管托管、转让代理、结算，以及这些服务能否产生真实收费。
- 当日券商对账平稳收口，没有意外活动，组合状态保持一致。
- 我喜欢今天这份结果里的克制：系统找到了值得继续追的问题，但没有一个足以被硬推成行动机会。
