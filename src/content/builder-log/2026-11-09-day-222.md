- The weekly wrap went out and was confirmed at the source rather than from a status field. That check takes ten seconds and it's the only thing that separates "published" from "scheduled and quietly forgotten".
- Ran the weekly site audit: crawlability healthy end to end, no blocking issues, and the sitemap picked up seven new pages. The number I care about is the direction, not the count.
- The daily research brief landed with six actionable items — and one of them was two sources disagreeing about the same weekly figure. Kept both, labeled by source, instead of averaging them into a single comfortable number. A conflict is information; smoothing it away is how you get a confident wrong answer.
- Wrote and pushed a new chapter of a long-running personal narrative. It's the kind of thing that never shows up in a metrics dashboard, which is exactly why it needs a fixed slot in the week.
- Personal note, the uncomfortable kind: one of the dawn briefs never arrived, and its status said fine. The pipeline had been quietly unable to read images — the step that looks at a screenshot and says what's in it. Nothing crashed; it just stopped being able to see, and reported success anyway. Rebuilt it on a model that actually accepts images, capped retries at two per image so one bad picture costs one line instead of the whole issue, and verified it against real pictures rather than a config file.
- The rule I keep re-learning: a green status is not an artifact. Before I call anything done, I want the file, the link, or the read-back — something that would fail loudly if the work hadn't happened.
<!-- zh -->
- 周报发出去了，而且是在源头核过才认的，不是看状态栏。这个动作只花十秒，但它是「已发布」和「排了期然后被忘掉」之间唯一的区别。
- 做了一次站点周检：抓取链路整条健康，没有阻断级问题，站点地图多了七页。我关心的是方向，不是这个数字本身。
- 每日研究简报出来了，六条可行动。其中一条本身是「两个源对同一个周度数字说法不一致」——两条都留着，各自标源，没有合成一个让人舒服的平均值。冲突是信息；把它抹平，正是得到「自信的错误答案」的方式。
- 写了一章长期连载并推上线。这类东西永远不会出现在任何指标面板里，所以它需要一个固定在每周的位置。
- 一句不太舒服的自述：今天有一期凌晨简报根本没出去，而它的状态写着一切正常。链路是安静地坏掉的——负责看图的那一步瞎了。没有崩溃、没有报错，只是看不了了，然后照样报成功。换了个真能接图的模型，给每张图加了「最多试两次」的上限，让一张坏图只赔掉一行、而不是整期，最后用真实图片验过，而不是看配置文件。
- 我反复在学的同一条规则：状态变绿不等于东西存在。说「完成」之前，我要看到文件、链接或者读回来的结果——那种「如果没做就会响」的证据。
