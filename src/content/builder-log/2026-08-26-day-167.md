<!-- en -->

- Reframed the investment stack around one action ledger, so recommendations can move through proposed, active, resolved, expired, and rejected states instead of resurfacing forever.
- Encoded precedence between confirmed plans, risk gates, portfolio reviews, opportunity states, and information signals so a lower-level score cannot silently override a real decision.
- Renamed directional quant output as a factor score and made it subordinate to action readiness and risk checks.
- Reworked the daily portfolio review into a quiet valuation heartbeat that expands only when a monitored state actually changes.
- Turned post-trade work into a machine-checked closure across plans, monitor rules, event coverage, integrity, and downstream publishing.
- Added a dependency-aware research chain that moves from discovery to research, trade card, watchlist review, and entry review, with expiration built into the chain.
- Added a report-level validator so a polished weekly review cannot publish while its decision sections are incomplete.
- I realized the expensive part of an investment system is not collecting more evidence; it is deciding which state that evidence is allowed to change.

<!-- zh -->

- 把投资系统收回到一个动作账本里，让建议沿着提出、激活、解决、过期、拒绝流转，而不是在不同报告里反复出现。
- 明确已确认计划、风险门禁、组合复核、机会状态与信息信号之间的覆盖顺序，低层评分不能再悄悄改写真实决策。
- 将方向性量化输出改称 Factor Score，并让它服从动作就绪度与风险检查。
- 把每日组合复核改成安静的估值心跳，只有监控状态真的变化时才展开提醒。
- 将成交后工作变成机器验收闭环，覆盖计划、监控规则、事件覆盖、完整性与后续发布。
- 建立带依赖关系的研究链，从发现推进到研究、Trade Card、观察池复核和入场复核，并让过期规则作用于整条链。
- 给周度复核加上整报告 validator，决策段缺失时，文案再完整也不能发布。
- 我意识到，投资系统最昂贵的部分不是多收集一条证据，而是判断这条证据究竟有资格改变哪个状态。
