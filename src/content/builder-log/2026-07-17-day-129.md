<!-- en -->

- I realized an old score can look precise long after the system behind it has stopped being useful, so the KOL review now starts from the original argument and timestamp.
- Reframed opinion tracking as a four-step chain: recent view, reasoning path, historical price evidence, and separate short- versus long-horizon accuracy.
- Verified portfolio changes against the canonical position source instead of trusting a write confirmation alone.
- Converted post-trade follow-up into a clearer discipline problem: risk limits and invalidation conditions must move with the position, not wait for a later report.
- Kept the morning portfolio view anchored to the canonical source when a secondary crypto monitor disagreed.
- Used three scheduled news windows to separate portfolio-relevant developments from the surrounding market feed.
- Rechecked stale IPO assumptions against current evidence rather than treating a task list as live state.
- The useful lesson today was simple: credibility is not a stored number; it is a path that another person can replay.

<!-- zh -->

- 我意识到，支撑系统早已失效后，一个旧分数仍然可以显得很精确，所以 KOL 评估现在从原始观点和时间戳重新开始。
- 观点跟踪被重构成四步：近期观点、推演路径、历史价格证据，以及短线和长线准确度分别验证。
- 持仓变化不再只信写入成功，而是回到唯一事实源读回确认。
- 交易后的问题也更清楚了：仓位变了，风险上限和失效条件就该一起变，不能等下一份报告再提醒。
- 当加密监控与事实源冲突时，早报继续以事实源为准，并把分叉明确留作修复项。
- 三个固定新闻窗口继续过滤与持仓真正相关的变化，不让市场信息流重新淹没决策。
- IPO 跟踪里的旧状态重新回到当前证据核验，没有让任务清单冒充实时现实。
- 今天最有用的认识很简单：信用不是一个存下来的数字，而是一条别人可以重新走一遍的证据路径。
