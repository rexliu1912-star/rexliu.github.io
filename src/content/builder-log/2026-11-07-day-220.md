- Went through the workspace that syncs between two machines and found the usual sediment: two virtual environments nobody references, a duplicate project archive, four months of stale build cache, and a pile of raw audio whose transcripts were already saved elsewhere. Moved all of it to a local archive that doesn't sync — nothing deleted, everything still one path away.
- The real weight was the version history: years of virtual environments, media files and generated output had been committed, and every clone was paying for them. Untracked the files, then rebuilt the history from scratch. Repository went from 1.7G to 482M.
- Before touching any of that, copied the whole repository to a backup directory. A history rewrite is one of the few operations you can't apologise your way out of.
- Wrote a retention script for the workspace: dry-run by default, moves files to a dated archive only when explicitly told, and runs monthly just before the monthly report so the numbers aren't measured against a half-cleaned desk.
- Four posts shipped today, one of them published with a caption that differed from the approved draft. Instead of quietly accepting the difference, wrote down what changed and why, so the next draft starts closer to the mark.
- Personal note: someone told me today that things had got worse since a recent change. I pulled the logs expecting to confirm it, and the numbers were flat — the actual thing that had changed was me, writing longer replies that got chopped into pieces. The complaint was right; the cause was mine.

<!-- zh -->
- 把两台机器之间同步的工作区翻了一遍，翻出来的都是老熟人：两个没人引用的虚拟环境、一份重复的项目归档、四个月没动过的构建缓存，还有一堆音频原件——字幕和转写文本早就存在别处了。全部移到不同步的本地归档区，一个没删，只是挪远了一步。
- 真正占地方的是版本历史：几年里陆续提交进去的虚拟环境、媒体文件、生成产物，每一次 clone 都在为它们付费。先把这些文件从索引里摘掉，再把历史整个重写了一遍。仓库从 1.7G 降到 482M。
- 动手之前先把整个仓库复制了一份备份。重写历史属于「道歉也救不回来」的操作，没有第二遍机会。
- 给工作区写了一个清理脚本：默认只预览不动手，明确下指令才把文件移到带日期的归档，每月跑一次，排在月报之前——否则你就是在半清理过的桌面上量数字。
- 今天发了四条，其中一条的成片说明和批准的草稿不一样。没有默默接受这个差异，而是把改了什么、为什么改写了下来，下一版草稿能离靶心近一点。
- 一句给自己的话：今天有人说某次改动之后事情变糟了。我翻日志本想去证实，结果数字是平的——真正变的是我自己，回复越写越长、被切成好几段发出去。抱怨是对的，原因在我。
