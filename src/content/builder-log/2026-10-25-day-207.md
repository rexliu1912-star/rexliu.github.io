- Reconnected drafting to the original voice notes and added a feedback path for published examples, substantial rewrites and abandoned drafts; I had nearly blamed the model before checking what it was actually reading.
- Narrowed the new data-writing experiment to X long posts and threads, with a weekly meme update as the editorial direction; the initial material remains a draft, not a new email publication.
- Added a public llms.txt and aligned the site's language metadata with its English default, making the existing site clearer to crawlers without starting another product.
- Took the next STICKERS ONLY episode from script to reviewable video, revised the community cast and captions, and handed the approved version to the editor.
- Defined a podcast-to-clip handoff: research supplies the source and suggested moments, the creative producer makes subtitled candidates, and publication still needs approval.
- Updated the weekly knowledge synthesis again after the day's journal arrived, so the late entry could inform existing principles and the personal trajectory instead of falling between reporting windows.
- Created one daily reading list linking the reports worth opening, with reminders reading that same list instead of maintaining another competing queue.
