<!-- en -->
- I caught myself treating a notification as a gate, when the final broker record was the better authority.
- We checked orders, fees, and positions together instead of trusting a single reassuring status.
- We kept a clean transaction ledger separate from the still-open question of decision quality.
- We carried a newer household asset snapshot through its source, historical record, and public presentation.
- We made missing statement coverage visible in the finance view rather than pretending a partial month was complete.
- We kept transfers between accounts out of spending and income so the totals describe real activity.
- We left investment performance unclaimed where comparable starting values and external cash flows were still missing.
- We tightened the earnings calendar around company disclosures, leaving fund symbols out of company-report coverage.

<!-- zh -->
- 我今天发现自己差点把一封进度通知当成门槛；真正有结算权的是券商的最终记录。
- 我们把订单、费用和仓位放在一起核对，不再相信一个好看的完成状态。
- 交易账对平了，决策质量是否过关仍是另一道题，没有顺手画等号。
- 新的家庭资产快照从原始来源一路更新到历史记录和公开页面。
- 财务页面直接标出缺失的账单来源，没有把不完整的月份装成完整。
- 账户之间的划转没有被算作消费或收入，让汇总回到真实活动。
- 缺少可比期初数据和外部资金流时，投资收益就暂时不下结论。
- 财报日历只跟公司披露走，基金符号不再混进公司财报范围。
