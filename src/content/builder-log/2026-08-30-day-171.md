- Fixed a weekend market-data edge case by separating an empty trailing candle from a genuinely missing quote, then verified the full downstream pipeline.
- Reframed the weekly asset review around conclusions, real performance, risk, and next actions instead of a procedural dump.
- Rebuilt the household asset snapshot from the current source and corrected a category that the previous model had silently omitted.
- Cut the main Notion and X tool instructions into short routing documents, moving deep references out of the high-frequency context.
- Archived two dormant research skills while keeping their useful knowledge and naming the active pipelines that now own the work.
- Removed two manual command shortcuts but preserved scheduled weekly planning, so subtraction did not break the underlying outcome.
- Let real content data strengthen an existing writing pattern without inventing a new label from a tiny sample.
- Absorbed four investment lessons into the standing framework: portfolio-level gates, one action ledger, factual trade lineage, and explicit decision attribution.
- My clearest realization today was that deleting a feature is easy; deleting ambiguity requires documenting its successor.

<!-- zh -->

- 修掉了一个周末行情边界问题：尾部空 K 线不再等于整只标的缺价，并把下游链路完整验收了一遍。
- 把周度资产复盘改成结论、真实表现、风险和下一步动作，不再堆执行流水。
- 用最新事实源重建家庭资产快照，同时补回旧模型静默漏掉的一类资产。
- 把 Notion 和 X 工具的主说明压成短路由，复杂细节全部移出高频上下文。
- 归档两个失活研究 Skill，同时保留有价值的知识，并明确现役管线继续承担哪些工作。
- 删除两个手动快捷命令，但保留自动周计划，让系统做减法时不丢结果。
- 内容数据只强化已有写作模式，没有因为样本太小就硬造一个新标签。
- 把四条投资教训写进正式框架：组合门禁、唯一动作账本、交易事实谱系和明确决策归因。
- 我今天最清楚的一点是：删功能很容易，真正难的是把它的继任者写清楚，连歧义一起删掉。
