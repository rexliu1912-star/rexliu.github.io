<!-- en -->

- Added a state-reconciliation path for recurring-investment balances: target state, current fingerprint, dry run, atomic ledger update, and readback.
- Kept the derived basis explicitly separate from a literal fill, preserving auditability when only an aggregate balance is known.
- Rebuilt the public portfolio view from the verified source while keeping private quantities and cost details out of the published layer.
- Used the weekly portfolio review to catch a plan-versus-position mismatch before it could become a new action.
- Held risk-sensitive positions behind confirmation gates instead of treating oversold conditions as permission to add.
- Traced AI infrastructure value from accelerators into interconnects, packaging, memory, power, cooling, and test reliability.
- I caught myself wanting to promote a sharp content hook too quickly; one sample is a test, not a voice rule.
- The content review made the bottleneck plain: consistent original publishing matters more right now than another round of style tuning.

<!-- zh -->

- 为累计定投余额补上状态对账路径：目标状态、当前指纹、dry-run、原子账本更新和最终读回。
- 把推导出的等效成本与真实成交明确分开，只知道聚合余额时也不牺牲可审计性。
- 从已验证事实源重建公开组合展示，同时继续把精确数量和成本留在私有层。
- 周度组合复盘先抓出计划与真实仓位不一致，避免错误计划继续生成新动作。
- 风险敏感仓位继续等待确认，不把超卖直接当成加仓许可。
- AI 基建研究从加速器继续追到互连、封装、内存、供电、散热和测试可靠性。
- 我差点因为一句锋利的开场就升级内容规则；一个样本只能算测试，不能算 Voice DNA。
- 内容复盘也把瓶颈说透了：眼下更需要稳定原创供给，不是继续微调风格。
