<!-- en -->

- Added a forward scorecard built from opportunities that were actually shown, so research quality is judged by later outcomes rather than confident presentation.
- Split trade discovery from settlement authority: one layer can notice an event quickly, while only canonical broker evidence is allowed to change the ledger.
- Hardened batch accounting so legitimate round trips can settle in order while any intermediate negative inventory still stops the write.
- Retired the duplicate machine-side planning surface and kept the human journal as the only place for deliberate pre-trade thinking and review.
- Removed public transaction replay from the portfolio site, leaving the interface focused on current exposure and active monitoring.
- I was reminded that the fastest input should not automatically become the source of truth; speed and authority are different design decisions.

<!-- zh -->

- 给真实展示过的机会补上 forward scorecard，让研究质量接受后续结果检验，而不是靠当时写得多有信心。
- 把成交发现与结算权分开：一层负责尽快看见事件，只有券商 canonical 证据才能改变账本。
- 收紧批量记账逻辑，合法的日内往返可以按顺序结算，任何中间负库存仍会立即停写。
- 退役重复的机器计划界面，只保留手工日志作为交易前思考与复盘的唯一入口。
- 从公开组合页移除历史交易回放，让界面回到当前暴露与现役监控。
- 我再次意识到，最快的输入不该自动成为事实源；速度与权威，本来就是两项不同的设计选择。
