<!-- en -->
- Rewrote the rule that governs the daily writing slot. Main line is now macro, crypto and AI; personal material from the journal is allowed when it's specific and true; and a weak topic means publish nothing that day. The part I keep thinking about is the last clause — an empty slot is not a failure, it's the rule working.
- Two of today's slots stayed empty and nobody rushed to fill them. That used to read to me like a hole in the schedule. Now it reads like a decision.
- Redesigned the report pages around readability: conclusions, actions and long explanations moved out of grey metadata and small print into the body, one idea per paragraph. The standard now lives in one place instead of being re-litigated per page.
- Built a file handoff between two machines that leaves receipts: identical hashes on both sides, an explicit manifest, and no automatic deletion at the source. The design choice I care about is the boring one — it refuses unknown inputs instead of guessing.
- Published three posts today, each confirmed at the source rather than from a status field. Ten seconds of checking is the only thing standing between "published" and "scheduled and quietly forgotten".
- Two sources disagreed about the same weekly figure again. Kept both, labeled by source, refused to average them into one comfortable number. A conflict is information; smoothing it away is how you earn a confident wrong answer.
<!-- zh -->
- 把日更那条规则重写了。主线改成宏观、加密和 AI；日记里的个人素材可以用，前提是具体、真实；题目弱就当天不发。我一直在想的是最后这半句——空槽不是失败，它是规则正常工作的样子。
- 今天两个槽位就空着，没人急着去填。以前我会把它看成日程表上的一个洞，现在我看成一次决定。
- 报告页按「读得下去」重做了一遍：结论、行动、长解释从灰色小字里搬回正文，一段只说一件事。标准现在收在一个地方，不用每张页再吵一遍。
- 给两台机器之间做了个带回执的文件交接：两边哈希一致、有明确清单、源头不自动删。我在意的设计反而最无趣——遇到不认识的输入它选择拒绝，而不是猜。
- 今天发了三条，每条都在源头核过，不是看状态栏。十秒钟的核对，是「已发布」和「排了期然后被忘掉」之间唯一的区别。
- 又有两个源对同一个周度数字说法不一样。两条都留着、各自标源，没有合成一个让人舒服的平均值。冲突本身是信息；把它抹平，就是得到「自信的错误答案」的方式。
