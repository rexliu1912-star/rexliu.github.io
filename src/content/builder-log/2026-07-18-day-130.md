<!-- en -->
- I changed the research question from “what else looks interesting?” to “which missing layer would change how I understand an existing position?”
- The genomics branch now points downstream to sample preparation and digital PCR, with QIAGEN as the first research card rather than another sequencing-platform story.
- The physical-AI branch now pairs sensing with execution, using Teradyne’s collaborative robotics business as a reality check on whether perception is becoming deployed work.
- The space branch now separates commercial launch and connectivity from defense-funded tracking and sensors, with L3Harris as the comparison case.
- I added an ETF-overlap gate and a falsification trigger before any new name earns deeper research; novelty alone is no longer enough.
- KOL evaluation now distinguishes industrial insight from trading timing, because someone can understand a cycle and still be consistently early on price.
- Upcoming earnings were converted into verification windows with named operating questions instead of vague calendar reminders.
- My personal reminder from today was simple: after an early mistake, slowing down and finishing the next step cleanly beats trying to win the lost time back.

<!-- zh -->
- 我把研究问题从“还有什么有意思”改成“哪一块缺失认知会改变我对现有持仓的理解”。
- 基因组学这条线继续往下游找，先研究样本前处理和数字 PCR，用 QIAGEN 做第一张深研卡，而不是再找一家测序平台。
- Physical AI 这条线把感知和执行配成一组，用 Teradyne 的协作机器人业务检验“看得见”是否真的变成了可部署的工作。
- 太空这条线把商业发射与连接，和国防预算驱动的跟踪卫星、传感器拆开，用 L3Harris 做对照。
- 新标的进入深研前新增两道门：ETF 是否已经覆盖，以及下一次什么数据能证伪；仅仅“新鲜”已经不够。
- KOL 评价开始区分产业判断和交易节奏，因为看懂周期的人，也可能一次次在价格上太早。
- 接下来的财报不再只是日历提醒，而是带着明确经营问题的验证窗口。
- 今天给自己的提醒很简单：前面失误后，放慢速度把下一步做干净，比急着追回丢掉的时间更有效。
