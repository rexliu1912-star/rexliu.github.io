<!-- en -->

- I chose one authoritative credentials path instead of keeping a second native integration just because it was available.
- The broader agent upgrade still kept the pieces that reduce friction: smarter approvals, delivery tracking, a smaller provider set, and clearer reasoning tiers.
- I completed a provincial population study covering cumulative change and annual direction across China from 2016 through 2024.
- The Xinjiang follow-up separated births, deaths, urbanization, migration, age structure, and population composition instead of forcing one neat explanation.
- I rebuilt KOL credibility checks around recent arguments, explicit reasoning chains, and real short- and long-window price evidence.
- Portfolio monitoring stayed action-bound: crossing a risk line creates a review, not an automatic trade or a convenient story.
- The recurring lesson was almost embarrassingly simple: a new layer is useful only when it removes ambiguity rather than creating another source of truth.

<!-- zh -->

- 我最后只留一条权威凭证读取路径，没有因为“原生支持”就保留第二套并行集成。
- 这次 Agent 升级仍保留了真正减少摩擦的部分：更聪明的审批、交付追踪、更小的 provider 集合和更清楚的 reasoning 分级。
- 完成全国省级人口研究，整理了 2016—2024 年累计变化与年度方向。
- 新疆部分没有硬凑一个漂亮答案，而是把出生、死亡、城镇化、迁移、年龄结构和人口构成拆开验证。
- KOL 信用检查改成最近观点、完整推演链和真实短长周期价格回溯，不再让旧分数假装精确。
- 组合监控继续绑定动作：越过风险线只触发复核，不自动变成交易，更不能顺手补一段自洽故事。
- 今天最朴素也最有用的判断是：新层只有在减少歧义时才算升级，否则只是多造一个事实源。
