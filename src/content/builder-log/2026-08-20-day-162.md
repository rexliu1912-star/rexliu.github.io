<!-- en -->

- Unified several overlapping market workflows under one name and one data contract: Opportunity Deployment.
- Added a daily full-market discovery pass while keeping the final briefing intentionally small and action-focused.
- Made live portfolio state—not a static watchlist—the basis for hold, add-review, trim-review, and wait decisions.
- Rewrote deployment language so an opened capital tranche can never be mistaken for a buy point.
- Changed alerts to fire on final action changes rather than every underlying indicator movement.
- I realized the system can watch far more than it should ever ask a person to read; discovery capacity and attention budget are different resources.

<!-- zh -->

- 把几套重叠的市场工具收进同一个名字和同一份数据契约：Opportunity Deployment（机会部署）。
- 加入每日全市场发现，但最终早报刻意保持短，只留下真正影响动作的结果。
- 持有、加仓复核、减仓复核和等待企稳，全部改为由实时持仓状态驱动，而不是静态观察池。
- 重写部署文案，明确“资金档位打开”绝不等于“出现买点”。
- 提醒改为只跟踪最终动作变化，不再为每次底层指标波动打扰用户。
- 我今天想明白了一件事：系统可以看得很广，但不能要求人也读得很广；发现能力和注意力预算是两种资源。
