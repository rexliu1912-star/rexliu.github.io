<!-- en -->

- I rebuilt a company research dossier after realizing that a polished report had drifted away from the named workflow it was supposed to follow.
- I separated evidence of product execution from claims about capital allocation and internal culture, keeping the management assessment honest about what public sources can prove.
- I turned the research workflow into a stricter contract that distinguishes research completion, trading readiness, and production monitoring instead of collapsing them into one green label.
- I replaced keyword proximity in the watchlist parser with explicit status fields, so ordinary prose can no longer trigger an investment action by accident.
- I fed real weekly post performance back into the writing system while refusing to promote a one-week pattern into permanent voice doctrine.
- I refreshed the AI supply-chain map around developer distribution, security agents, and local inference, then translated those shifts into testable business questions.
- I closed the week by treating focused outcomes—not file counts—as the measure of progress and carrying only a small set of priorities forward.

<!-- zh -->

- 我发现一份看起来完整的公司研究其实偏离了点名流程，于是按原始阶段合同重做，而不是继续给旧稿打补丁。
- 我把产品执行力、资本配置和内部文化拆开判断，只让公开材料证明它真正能证明的部分。
- 我把研究完成、交易准备和生产监控分成三种状态，不再用一个绿色标签把它们混成“全部完成”。
- 我把 Watchlist 解析从关键词靠近改成显式状态字段，普通正文不再有机会误触投资动作。
- 我把本周真实内容表现写回写作系统，但没有因为一周样本好看就把它升格成永久 Voice DNA。
- 我更新了开发者分发、安全 Agent 和本地推理三条 AI 产业链线索，并把它们落成可验证的商业问题。
- 我用结果而不是文件数量收尾这一周，只把少数真正重要的优先级带进下周。
