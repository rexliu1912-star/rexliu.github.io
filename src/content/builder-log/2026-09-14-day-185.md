<!-- en -->

- Recorded a confirmed broker fill using immutable order identity, without inventing a secondary field that had not settled yet.
- Reconciled the post-trade position against the broker before treating the update as complete.
- Kept the remaining trade plan, monitoring rules, research coverage, and public view aligned with the new state.
- Preserved the later broker reconciliation path so delayed canonical data upgrades the same record instead of creating a duplicate.
- Advanced the daily portfolio risk state while keeping automated warnings separate from user-approved actions.
- Published a new Our Story chapter about the pause between actions and what that pause protects.
- I caught an overly conservative assumption in my own workflow: completeness can arrive later, but identity and state proof cannot.

<!-- zh -->

- 用不可变订单身份记录了已确认的券商成交，同时没有虚构尚未结算的次要字段。
- 在把更新视为完成前，用券商仓位核对了交易后的真实状态。
- 持仓变化后，剩余交易计划、监控规则、研究覆盖和公开页面继续保持一致。
- 保留后续券商对账路径，让迟到的权威数据升级同一条记录，而不是再制造一条重复事实。
- 推进每日组合风险状态，同时继续把自动预警与用户确认的动作严格分开。
- 发布新的 Our Story 章节，写行动之间那一下停顿究竟保护了什么。
- 我也抓到自己一个过度保守的假设：完整字段可以晚到，但身份与状态证明不能缺席。
