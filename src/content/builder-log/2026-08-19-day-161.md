<!-- en -->

- Rebuilt the drawdown deployment view into two explicit layers: representative indexes decide whether a market is in a deployment zone, while individual securities decide how to express that view.
- Kept weekly defense levels and tranche sizing as separate parameters, so a presentation change could not silently alter execution logic.
- Replaced improvised report formatting with a stable formatter, clear position and candidate markers, and direct instrument links.
- Shifted part of the portfolio away from concentrated growth exposure toward broader-market and energy diversification without treating the rebalance as a reason to chase prices.
- Marked an entry outside the existing research gate as a discipline exception, preserved its risk boundaries, and refused to rewrite the thesis after the trade.
- Distilled a useful AI infrastructure reminder from today’s research: the next phase needs earnings delivery, not another round of valuation expansion.
- I was reminded that a pipeline can be technically green and still fail at the value layer if the person reading it cannot tell what market state changed or what action follows.

<!-- zh -->

- 把大跌部署重做成两个明确层级：代表性指数判断市场是否进入部署区，个股和 ETF 只负责回答如何实现这层判断。
- 周线防线与资金分档继续保持为两组独立参数，避免一次展示改版悄悄改掉执行逻辑。
- 不再让 Agent 临场拼报告，改用固定格式器、清晰的持仓与候选标记，以及可直接打开的行情链接。
- 组合从高集中成长敞口向宽基与能源分散了一部分，但没有把再平衡当成追价的理由。
- 一笔越过既有研究门槛的入场被明确标为纪律例外，风险边界保留，也没有在成交后重写故事替它辩护。
- 今天的研究又确认了一件事：AI 基建下一阶段要靠盈利兑现，而不是继续抬估值。
- 我再次意识到，管线技术上全绿仍可能在价值层失败——如果读者看不出哪个市场状态变了、下一步该做什么，系统就还没完成。
