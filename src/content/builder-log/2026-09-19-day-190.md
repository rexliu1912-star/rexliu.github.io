- Unified precious metals, crypto, U.S. equities, China A-shares, and Hong Kong equities under one opportunity producer instead of adding more dashboards.
- Reduced the visible strategy vocabulary to two setups—breakout and reversal—and ranked candidates by actionability rather than category quotas.
- Added Hong Kong discovery as an isolated lane, so a regional data failure cannot degrade healthy results from the other markets.
- Mapped the stablecoin value chain across issuers, exchanges, payment processors, brokers, banks, and asset managers instead of treating every participant as the same exposure.
- Separated structural demand for short-term Treasuries from the much harder questions of deficits, long-duration supply, and fiscal sustainability.
- Consolidated the daily journal into one canonical template shared by manual writing and morning preparation, with related records linked only when they actually exist.
- I realized that broader coverage should make the final interface narrower, not larger: discovery can scale in the background while attention remains scarce.

<!-- zh -->

- 把贵金属、加密、美股、A 股和港股收进同一个机会 producer，没有再增加新的仪表盘。
- 可见策略只保留突破与反转两类，并按可执行性排序，不再用类别配额决定谁能上卡。
- 把港股发现做成隔离通道，单一区域的数据问题不会拖累其他健康市场的结果。
- 拆开稳定币产业链里的发行、交易、支付、经纪、银行与资管角色，不再把所有参与者当成同一种暴露。
- 区分短端美债的结构性新增需求，与赤字、长债供给和财政可持续性这些更难的问题。
- 把每日 Journal 收敛成手工写作与晨间准备共用的唯一模板，相关记录只在真实存在时链接。
- 我今天意识到，覆盖越广，最终界面反而应该越窄：后台发现可以扩张，人的注意力仍然必须稀缺。
