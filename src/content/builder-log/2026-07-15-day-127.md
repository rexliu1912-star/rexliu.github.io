<!-- en -->

- I stopped treating a long course list as a learning plan and rebuilt options study around minimum knowledge, real trade reviews, one strategy at a time, and paper trading.
- I made the risk boundary explicit: start with defined-risk structures and avoid naked selling or ultra-short-dated speculation while the process is still untested.
- I archived a practical study guide with core concepts, strategy references, review prompts, and a trade-journal path instead of another pile of bookmarks.
- I reduced the podcast feed to a smaller verified set, adding one strong Chinese investing source and removing subscriptions that no longer earned attention.
- I adjusted community filtering so primary voices are not discarded merely because every post does not repeat the project keyword.
- I hardened the reading sync so unrelated generated data no longer blocks fresh library updates.
- The useful lesson today was personal: information becomes knowledge only when it produces a card, a simulation, a decision, or a review.

<!-- zh -->

- 我不再把一长串课程当成学习计划，而是把期权学习改成最小知识、真实交易复盘、一次只练一个策略，再进入模拟交易。
- 风险边界也写清楚了：流程还没验证前，只碰最大亏损明确的结构，不做裸卖，也不碰超短期限投机。
- 我把核心知识、策略资源、复盘问题和交易日记路径整理成一份实战指南，而不是再堆一批书签。
- 播客源缩成一组更小且全部验证过的清单，加入一个优质中文投资源，也删掉不再值得占用注意力的订阅。
- 社区过滤规则做了修正，核心声音不会再因为每条内容都没重复项目关键词而被误删。
- 阅读同步链路也加固了，无关的生成数据不再阻塞新的书架更新。
- 今天最有用的体会很个人：信息只有变成一张卡、一次模拟、一个决定或一轮复盘，才真正成了知识。
