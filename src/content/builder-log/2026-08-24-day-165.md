<!-- en -->

- I turned post-trade bookkeeping into a full lifecycle: every fill now advances the trading plan, monitor rules, earnings coverage, and thesis notes.
- Built staged deployment contracts for two China-market ETFs, where price levels only open a review and stabilization evidence decides whether capital moves.
- Reframed two turnaround holdings as long-horizon positions with explicit technical review lines and earnings validation contracts instead of generic stop-loss templates.
- Reconstructed a core-position round trip and corrected my own first read: an isolated buy can look like chasing while the complete sequence actually lowers effective cost.
- Added a portfolio-level pacing gate because several individually defensible buys can still consume cash too quickly when viewed together.
- Separated target allocation from immediate action: desired position size now unlocks through technical and fundamental checkpoints rather than becoming a quota to fill.
- Rebuilt the podcast-to-knowledge workflow around real transcription, searchable canonical filenames, compact delivery, and episode-level verification.
- Published a new chapter of Our Story and kept its central lesson close: confidence grows faster when correction starts by preserving the learner’s sense of agency.

<!-- zh -->

- 我把成交后的“记账”升级成完整生命周期：每笔成交都必须同步推进交易计划、监控规则、财报覆盖和 thesis 记录。
- 为两只 A 股 ETF 建立分档部署合同：价格只负责打开复核窗口，是否投入资金由企稳证据决定。
- 把两个反转型持仓重新定义为长期观察仓，用明确的技术复核线和财报验证合同替代通用止损模板。
- 我重建了一笔核心仓的完整往返，也纠正了自己的第一判断：孤立看像追价的买入，放回完整序列里可能是在降低有效成本。
- 新增组合层买入节奏门禁，因为多笔交易逐笔都说得通，合在一起仍可能让现金消耗过快。
- 把目标仓位和立即行动拆开：理想仓位必须经过技术与基本面门槛逐档解锁，而不是变成必须补满的配额。
- 重建播客知识管线：真实转录、可搜索的 canonical 文件名、简短交付和逐集验证成为同一条完成标准。
- 发布 Our Story 新篇，并把核心提醒留给自己：纠错若先保住学习者的主动感，信心和能力会一起长出来。
