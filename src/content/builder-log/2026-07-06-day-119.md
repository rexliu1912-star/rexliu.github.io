<!-- en -->

- Reconnected a private daily-recording loop and treated the big recap as a warning sign: if a system needs a heroic recovery review, the normal input path is probably too heavy.
- The strongest product lesson today was simple: a personal AI system should make important low-stimulation habits easier, not just generate better summaries after the fact.
- The market pipeline kept a clean distinction between a strong long-term memory story and poor short-term chase conditions.
- The AI infrastructure frame moved another layer down into physical constraints: power access, grid scheduling, memory supply, and storage demand now matter as much as the headline chip story.
- A research paper on flexible grid connection turned into a useful investing lens: data centers can adapt workloads, but that does not make energy constraints disappear.
- The family-learning archive caught a real progress day and wrote it into the right structured paths instead of letting it disappear into chat history.
- The nightly maintenance loop also cleaned the memory metadata itself, removing stale undated warnings rather than carrying them forward.

<!-- zh -->

- 今天把一个私密的日常记录闭环重新接上，也顺手确认了一件事：如果系统需要靠一次大复盘来补救，说明平时的输入入口太重了。
- 最重要的产品判断很朴素：个人 AI 系统不该只会事后总结，它应该让那些重要但不刺激的日常习惯更容易发生。
- 投资管线今天守住了边界：长期内存逻辑可以很强，短线追涨条件不够就是不追。
- AI 基建框架又往现实世界沉了一层：电力接入、电网调度、内存供给和存储需求，已经和芯片本身同样重要。
- 一篇关于弹性并网的论文变成了新的投资视角：数据中心可以调度负载，但能源约束不会因为调度存在就消失。
- 家庭学习档案今天抓住了一个真实进步日，并写进正确的结构化路径，而不是让它留在聊天记录里。
- 夜间维护也顺手清理了记忆元数据本身，把过期的无日期警告修掉，而不是每天继续背着噪音往前跑。
