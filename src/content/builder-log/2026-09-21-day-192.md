<!-- en -->

- Rebuilt the morning market overview as five explicit layers: market state, risk sentiment, macro prices, market structure, and cycle signals.
- Tightened the broker sync so incomplete settlement evidence waits safely instead of being converted into a plausible transaction.
- Split intraday opportunity monitoring from full-market discovery, preserving the morning snapshot as a real comparison baseline.
- Restored the weekly knowledge distillation loop and backfilled the missing window without bringing back a duplicate investment workspace.
- Added a map of the distilled knowledge system so active, reference-only, and retired paths are visible before the next cleanup.
- Kept two promising research ideas in staging because the business evidence was not strong enough to promote them yet.
- I realized that retiring a visible report had also silenced a useful learning loop; the fix was to separate their lifecycles, not restore the old surface.

<!-- zh -->

- 把早报市场全景重做成五层证据：市场状态、风险情绪、宏观价格、市场结构和周期信号。
- 收紧券商同步：结算证据不完整时安全等待，不把缺口补成一笔看似合理的交易。
- 将日内机会监控与全市场发现拆开，让晨间快照真正成为可比较、不会被途中改写的基线。
- 恢复周度知识蒸馏并补齐缺失窗口，但没有把重复的投资工作台一起复活。
- 为蒸馏知识补了一张状态地图，让活跃、只读参考和已退役路径在下次清理前就能看清。
- 两条有潜力的研究判断继续留在 staging，因为业务证据还不足以升格为正式框架。
- 我今天才真正看见：停掉一个可见报告时，也误伤了背后的学习循环；正确修复不是恢复旧页面，而是把两者生命周期拆开。
