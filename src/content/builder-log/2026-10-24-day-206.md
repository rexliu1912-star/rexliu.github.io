<!-- en -->
- Compared independent English and Chinese drafts from the same brief; I wanted to see which version sounded like something I had actually lived, rather than reward the most polished generality.
- Gave outward-facing English copy a clear handoff: one writer prepares the draft, an editor checks the voice and plans it, and publishing stays behind approval.
- Replaced a borrowed-style exercise with learning from my own edits, so rejected lines and rewrites become useful evidence for the next draft.
- Expanded the existing creative producer's scope to images, video and scripts instead of adding another specialist with an overlapping job.
- Revised the next STICKERS ONLY script with recognizable community members, everyday rituals and a clearer invitation for viewers to take part; it is still a script, not a finished episode.
- Put finished reports and drafts under one output tree while leaving personal notes, references and long-term frameworks in their own places.
- Assigned product changelogs and feature videos to one owner, with a simple rule: bring me meaningful changes and stay quiet when nothing has changed.

<!-- zh -->
- 同一份素材分别做了英文和中文独立试稿；我想看哪一版像自己真经历过的事，不想把票投给最漂亮的空话。
- 对外英文文案有了清楚的交接：写稿的人出初稿，编辑审口吻和排计划，发布仍留在审批之后。
- 撤掉借别人文风练习的做法，回到我自己的改稿，让被删的句子和重写的过程成为下一稿的依据。
- 把图文、视频和剧本归给现有创意制作角色，没有再加一个职责重叠的新角色。
- 下一集 STICKERS ONLY 剧本加入了能认出来的社区成员、日常仪式和更清楚的参与入口；目前只是剧本，还不是成片。
- 报告和初稿统一进一棵产出目录，个人笔记、参考材料和长期框架仍各归原位。
- 给产品更新日志和功能视频定了一个负责人：有实质变化再告诉我，没变化就安静。
