<!-- en -->

- Changed crypto opportunity degradation from a global switch to per-asset isolation, so one missing history series no longer blinds the whole market view.
- Kept market-wide action restrictions for the only cases that justify them: a non-live shared snapshot or no usable history across the shortlist.
- Prevented degraded payloads from entering the performance record as if they had produced verified execution-ready signals.
- Re-ran the full quality gate and confirmed that the frozen morning presentation stayed unchanged while the underlying source health recovered.
- Hardened the portfolio-news intake with bounded retry, fail-closed behavior, and a recovery pass that backfills the missing window before declaring the queue healthy.
- Refreshed the daily investment workbench and gold-allocation dashboard from their canonical evidence layers.
- I realized that “safe” is not the same as “empty”: the better system keeps every verified fact moving while stopping incomplete evidence at the action boundary.

<!-- zh -->

- 把加密机会链从全局降级改成逐资产隔离：一条历史序列缺失，不再让整张市场视图一起失明。
- 只有共享市场快照不再实时，或候选池全部缺少可用历史时，才允许收紧整条链的动作权限。
- 阻止降级产物进入绩效历史，避免它们被当成已经产生过可执行信号的正常样本。
- 重新跑完质量门禁，确认底层来源恢复时，冻结的晨间展示没有被途中改写。
- 为持仓新闻入口补上有限重试、持续失败关闭和断档回补：先补齐空窗、完成去重，再宣布队列健康。
- 从各自的权威证据层刷新每日投资工作台和黄金配置页面。
- 我今天真正想清楚了：安全不等于空白。更好的系统会让每条已验证事实继续流动，只把不完整证据挡在动作边界之外。
