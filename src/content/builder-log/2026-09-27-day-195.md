<!-- en -->
- I used to treat a red audit as a blocked report; today I saw that a clearly labeled red report can be the more useful product.
- We taught a weekly review to pass validation only when unresolved differences remain explicit.
- We kept the action layer closed while the underlying records still disagree.
- We separated fast discovery from authoritative reconciliation instead of asking one source to play both roles.
- A content review showed that a stronger publishing week is not proof that one sentence length wins.
- An industry review separated product certification, shipping hardware, prototypes, and customer orders into different evidence stages.

<!-- zh -->
- 我以前容易把审计红灯等同于报告不能发；今天发现，标清红灯的报告反而更有用。
- 我们让周报在差异明确标为未解决时，也能通过降级验收。
- 底层记录没对齐之前，动作层仍然关着。
- 快速发现和权威对账各做各的，不让一个来源同时扮演两个角色。
- 内容复盘提醒我：发文恢复不等于某种句长被证明更有效。
- 产业链复盘把产品认证、上市硬件、技术原型和客户订单拆成不同证据阶段。
