- Added a dual-market closed-state guard so portfolio snapshots cannot masquerade as live intraday moves.
- Cross-checked a zero-content week across independent sources, then refused to update the writing model without fresh evidence.
- Turned the content gap into a measured restart plan instead of a backlog dump or a burst of catch-up posts.
- Traced AI infrastructure financing as a new research layer while keeping nonbinding announcements separate from signed demand.
- Kept optical-interconnect chatter in the research queue until orders, yields, and margins provide harder proof.
- Consolidated this week's durable AI and macro lessons into their canonical framework files and cleared the staging queue.
- I noticed that a beautifully organized list can still hide a refusal to choose, so active focus now starts with outcomes, not category quotas.
