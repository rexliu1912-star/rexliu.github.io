<!-- en -->
- We retired four investment discovery routines and their dedicated scripts instead of leaving another layer of paused machinery behind.
- We paused the dependent brief and opportunity reminders so removing the producers did not leave their consumers asking for missing data.
- We kept transaction reconciliation, shared market data, calendars, and historical evidence separate from the retired discovery work.
- We removed the journal review and omission audit from the evening report, leaving completed work and timely reminders for tomorrow.
- I realized that a report can be more personal when it asks fewer questions, rather than filling every slot with something that sounds useful.
- We allowed the reminder list to stay empty and kept research at zero actionable recommendations when the evidence did not justify more.

<!-- zh -->
- 四条投资发现流程和专用脚本正式退役，没有再留一层暂停的机器。
- 依赖它们的简报与机会提醒同步停用，避免生产端删了，消费端还继续索要数据。
- 成交对账、共享行情、日历和历史证据独立保留，没有跟着发现流程一起撤掉。
- 晚报取消日志复盘和遗漏审计，只留下今日完成与真正有时效的明日提醒。
- 我今天意识到，更贴合自己的报告，有时不是填更多栏，而是少问几个已经不再需要的问题。
- 提醒允许为空；证据不足时，研究也可以不给任何可执行推荐。
