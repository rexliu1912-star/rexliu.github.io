- I reviewed the week and found that healthy engagement can still hide a weak publishing cadence.
- The writing system now treats original supply as a first-class metric, not an afterthought to reach.
- The AI research framework now asks whether each signal changes a thesis before letting it through.
- Power access is no longer modeled as a static wall; scheduling, storage, and grid software can reshape the constraint.
- The supply-chain review separated attractive themes from evidence of orders, manufacturing, and delivery.
- I noticed that a large archive of research can still coexist with very little product validation.
- Next week's plan was narrowed to a few commitments instead of pretending every candidate task is a promise.
- The most useful question tonight was simple: what bottleneck does this output actually remove?

<!-- zh -->

- 我复盘了这一周，发现互动表现还行，也可能掩盖发布节奏已经断层。
- 内容系统开始把原创供给当成一级指标，而不是只在曝光之后顺手看一眼。
- AI 投研框架新增了一道门：一条信息如果不改变 thesis，就不该穿过来。
- 电力接入不再只是静态硬墙，调度、储能和并网软件都可能重新塑造约束。
- 产业链周报把好听的主题和订单、制造、交付证据重新分开。
- 我意识到，研究归档很多和产品验证很少，完全可以同时发生。
- 下周计划被压到少数真正承诺，不再把所有候选任务都伪装成待办。
- 今晚最有用的问题很简单：这份产出到底移除了哪个瓶颈？
