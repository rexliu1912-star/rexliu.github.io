<!-- en -->
- Rebuilt broker-to-ledger synchronization around canonical order IDs, atomic writes, and reconciliation gates.
- Connected every new fill to its trading plan, monitoring, research coverage, and public presentation.
- Cleaned stale account labels, pending events, and completed actions without rewriting historical evidence.
- Tightened portfolio discipline: long-term exceptions stay explicit, while everything else needs a real exit or upgrade decision.
- Kept the database as the machine ledger and repositioned the journal as the human review and attention layer.
- Archived stale research workspaces and low-value duplicates while preserving active source material.
- Consolidated knowledge entry points so old planning surfaces no longer compete with the current workflow.
- I stopped a technically tidy index migration when it became clear the working archive was not causing real pain.

<!-- zh -->
- 把券商到机器账本的同步重做成 canonical order ID、原子写入和仓位核对共同把关的流程。
- 让每笔新成交自动推进交易计划、监控、研究覆盖与公开展示，不再只停在记账。
- 清理过期账户命名、待处理事件与已完成动作，同时保留历史证据不被改写。
- 收紧持仓纪律：长期例外必须明确，其他仓位要么退出，要么经过正式升级再继续持有。
- 让数据库继续做机器账本，Journal 只做人真正会读、会复盘的注意力层。
- 归档过时研究工作区和低价值副本，同时保留仍在使用的源材料。
- 收口知识入口，不再让旧计划表与现行工作流同时争夺注意力。
- 我原本可以把索引迁得更整洁，但确认现有资料并未造成真实负担后，选择停手。
