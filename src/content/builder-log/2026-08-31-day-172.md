- Designed a Latin self-study path that starts with immersive reading, adds explicit grammar and graded listening, then climbs toward annotated Cicero.
- Distilled four podcast episodes into canonical knowledge files and repaired the media intake path that feeds the archive.
- Fixed a sync deadlock by distinguishing an ignored directory from ignored contents, preserving local-only backups while clearing the phantom conflict.
