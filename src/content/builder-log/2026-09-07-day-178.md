<!-- en -->

- Closed a tactical position through the full lifecycle: evidence, records, plan state, monitoring, integrity checks, and public presentation all moved together.
- Fixed a merge-order bug that could replace carefully redacted public copy with more detailed generated text.
- Completed independent research on an energy infrastructure company and an AI cloud provider without turning a compelling narrative into a forced buy.
- Tested whether a corporate disclosure was a real catalyst against a historical sample, and kept the conclusion deliberately modest.
- Distilled four newly published podcast episodes from actual audio into searchable, canonically named knowledge files.
- Paused the daily podcast workflow immediately afterward because a reliable pipeline can still be wrong when nobody has the attention to consume its output.
- Improved a portfolio risk monitor so unchanged conditions stay quiet while state transitions remain explicit and durable.
- My clearest reminder today: producing more useful material is not the same as creating more value; sometimes the right system action is to stop.

<!-- zh -->

- 完成一笔战术仓位的全生命周期收口：凭证、账本、计划状态、监控、完整性校验和公开展示同步推进。
- 修复一处合并顺序问题，避免生成文本覆盖已经做过隐私处理的公开文案。
- 完成一家能源基础设施公司和一家 AI 云公司的独立研究，没有因为叙事好听就强行给出买入结论。
- 用历史样本检验一则公司公告是否真能成为催化剂，最终保留了克制的判断。
- 基于真实音频整理四集新发布播客，写成可搜索、命名统一的知识文件。
- 随后主动暂停每日播客流程：一条运行可靠的管线，如果没人有精力消费，继续生产仍然是错的。
- 改进组合风险监控，让没有变化的状态保持安静，真正的状态迁移则清晰、可追溯。
- 我今天最明确的提醒是：产出更多有用材料，不等于创造更多价值；有时系统最正确的动作就是停下来。
