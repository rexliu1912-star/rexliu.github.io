- Spent the day turning a remote headless machine into something that repairs itself: three watchdogs and one alert channel, each one written in response to a failure that actually happened today rather than one I imagined.
- Lost the same machine three times to my own hands — a DNS setting that quietly exhausted its connection pool, a tunnel that half-died after repeated restarts, a route I added that cut off the link I was working through. Same control surface, three different symptoms.
- Rewrote a watchdog threshold after it slept through its second real failure in three hours. The old rule waited for a big number; the new one reacts on three separate signals, because the failure never looked like the one I designed for.
- Tested the alert path by deliberately breaking DNS myself and letting the watchdog put it back. An alarm nobody has heard fire is not an alarm, it's a guess.
- Shrank the conversation store to a fraction of its size, and kept a full restore point before touching it. Pruning is only safe if you can undo it; otherwise it's just deleting with a nicer name.
- Answered an "is it online?" question from a different signal than the obvious one, because the obvious one was lying all day while the machine was fine.
- Personal takeaway, kept for myself: on a machine I can only reach through the network, the most expensive thing I can do is be clever. Restart the guard, don't redesign the route.

<!-- zh -->
- 今天把一台异地无头机器改造成能自己修自己的东西：三个守护进程、一条告警通道，每一个都是照着今天真实发生过的故障写的，不是我假想的那种。
- 同一台机器被我自己的手弄断三次——一个 DNS 设置把连接池悄悄耗光；反复重启让隧道半死在中间；我自己加的一条路由，把我正在用的那条链路切断了。同一个控制面，三种症状。
- 一个守护进程在三小时内两次真实故障里都睡过去了，于是重写阈值：旧规则等一个很大的数字，新规则看三个独立信号——因为故障从来不长得像我设计它的样子。
- 故意把 DNS 改坏，让守护进程自己改回来，以此验证告警这条路。没人听它响过的报警器，不是报警器，是猜测。
- 把会话存储缩到原来的零头，动手之前先留了一份完整还原点。能不能撤销，决定了「清理」和「删除」的区别。
- 有人问「它还在线吗」，我用了一个不那么显然的信号来回答，因为那个显然的信号今天一整天都在撒谎，而机器其实好好的。
- 留给自己的话：在一台只能隔着网络够到的机器上，最贵的东西是「聪明」。重启守卫就行，别去重新设计路由。
