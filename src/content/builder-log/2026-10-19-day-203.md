<!-- en -->
- Tested recovery of old saved changes in an isolated workspace before clearing them; I would rather prove I can undo a cleanup than celebrate a shorter file list.
- Consolidated shared workspace rules and retired stale entry points, keeping historical material indexed rather than letting it masquerade as current instructions.
- Gave morning and evening reports separate archive identities, with matching readers and audit files, so one day's output can preserve both editions.
- Exercised a read-only coding-assistant handoff through the local bot workflow without changing the workspace.
- Turned a community-and-belief essay into a bright, dense English info card with a full-resolution download, alongside a separate short English draft.
- Transcribed a long-form economics discussion and saved a searchable summary with listening timestamps, keeping the speaker's hypotheses separate from verified facts.
- Published a new Our Story chapter, continuing the long-running narrative without turning it into another work report.

<!-- zh -->
- 先在独立工作区逐份恢复旧改动，确认可还原后再清理；比起文件列表短了多少，我更在意能不能撤回这次清理。
- 收拢跨工具共享规则，退役过时入口，历史材料保留索引，不再混成当前指令。
- 晨报和晚报分别归档，读取端与审计文件同步改好，让同一天的两个版本都能留下。
- 通过本地Bot流程跑通编码助手的只读交接，工作区没有被修改。
- 把一篇社区与信念的文章做成明亮、高密度英文信息卡，保留高清下载，另写一份简短英文草稿。
- 转录一场长篇经济讨论，留下可搜索摘要和回听时间戳，把讲者假说与已核实事实分开。
- 发布Our Story新章，继续长篇叙事，不把它写成另一份工作汇报。
