- The day's real work was custody of numbers. Four different views of the same portfolio existed — a fund-platform snapshot, a broker's paper statement, exchange balances, and a local file someone had rebuilt by hand. Each was individually believable; together they were four opinions. Now there is one path: read the account, recompute the buckets, rebuild the page, and audit for private data on the way out.
- The part that mattered wasn't the new total, it was the mapping. Two exchange-traded funds had been silently filed under A-shares, which made the domestic exposure look larger and the overseas one smaller than it was. Nothing errored. A correctly-labelled wrong classification is the most expensive kind of bug: every downstream chart inherits it and none of them complain.
- The travel page shipped with five photos and a map. What it taught me: the same visible symptom — a sideways photo — had nothing to do with the camera. The image carried a rotation flag, and the browser cheerfully obeyed a second time. Strip the metadata, correct the two real ones, done.
- Two more bugs hid behind that page. Styles scoped to the component never reached images injected later by script, so the frames only worked for the server-rendered ones. And a map that auto-opened a city on a timer kept stealing the choice the reader had already made. Both are the same mistake in different clothes: code that assumes it is the only thing touching state.
- Also moved the team's routine schedule back onto our own machine — eighteen jobs off the external scheduler, each one read back after being written, because "the call succeeded" and "the job is scheduled" are different sentences. A role was removed from the active roster the same evening; its records and history stayed.
- And a collector that had failed four days running got fixed by deleting work instead of adding retries. Four causes stacked, but the honest bottleneck was one optional section that structurally could never finish inside the budget. It now runs in roughly half the time and covers almost everything instead of claiming everything.
- What I keep from today: when two sources disagree, don't pick a winner — find out which one is a copy. And when a job can't finish, the first question isn't how to make it faster, it's which part of it was never going to succeed.

<!-- zh -->
- 今天真正花力气的是数字的归属。同一份组合在不同地方有四个说法——基金平台的快照、券商的纸质对账单、交易所余额、还有一份本地手搓的文件。单看每个都成立，放一起就是四种意见。现在只剩一条路：读账户、重算桶、重建页面、出门前再查一遍有没有泄露隐私。
- 关键不在新的总数，在映射。两只场内 ETF 一直被悄悄算进了 A 股，于是国内敞口显得比实际大、海外显得比实际小。全程没有任何报错。标签正确但归类错误，是最贵的一类 bug：所有下游图表都继承了它，而它们没有一个会抱怨。
- 旅行页上线了，五张照片加一张地图。它教我的事：可见症状一样，原因可以完全无关。照片躺着，跟相机没关系——文件里带了个旋转标记，浏览器很听话地又转了一次。清掉元数据、把真正拍歪的两张校正，就好了。
- 页面背后还藏着两个。组件的样式作用域管不到后来由脚本注入的图片，于是只有服务端渲染那几张有相框；地图则会在几秒后自动打开一个城市，把读者已经选好的那个抢掉。两个其实是同一个错误换了件衣服：代码默认自己是唯一在改状态的人。
- 还把团队的例行调度搬回了自己的机器——十八条任务从外部调度器迁走，每条都要读回确认，因为「调用成功了」和「任务真在排期里」是两句不同的话。同一个晚上撤掉了一个角色，记录和历史都留着。
- 另有一个连挂四天的采集任务，靠删活修好，而不是加更多重试。四个原因叠在一起，但真正的瓶颈是一段可选环节——它在时间预算里结构上永远做不完。现在耗时大约减半，覆盖几乎全部，而不是宣称覆盖全部。
- 今天留下的两句：两个来源打架时，别选赢家，先搞清哪个是副本。任务做不完时，第一个问题不是怎么更快，而是哪一部分本来就注定做不成。
