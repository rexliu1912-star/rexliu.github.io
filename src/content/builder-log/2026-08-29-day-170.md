<!-- en -->

- Reviewed months of investment decisions to separate profitable luck from behavior that can actually be repeated.
- Rebuilt the review around four distinct roles: strategy, agent advice, confirmed controls, and real execution.
- Stopped treating unconfirmed suggestions as user discipline and stopped applying newer rules retroactively.
- Moved decision ownership and confirmation into the action ledger instead of leaving them in prose reports.
- Bound every completed trade to its originating decision so future reviews can follow an immutable history.
- Migrated representative historical actions through the new lineage and verified the full lifecycle.
- Matched reasoning depth to task complexity and shortened research cache freshness without adding another user-facing workflow.
- My clearest lesson today: judgment is only fair after attribution is complete.

<!-- zh -->

- 回看数月投资决策，把“赚到了”与“真正可复制”拆成两套证据。
- 用四个独立角色重做复盘：策略、Agent 建议、已确认控制和真实执行。
- 不再把未确认建议算成用户纪律，也不拿后来的规则追溯评价旧交易。
- 把决策归属和确认状态写进动作账本，不再只留在复盘文字里。
- 每笔已执行交易都绑定原始决策，让未来复盘能沿不可变历史追溯。
- 用代表性历史动作跑通新谱系，并验证完整生命周期。
- 按任务复杂度分配推理深度，同时缩短研究缓存时效，没有新增用户入口。
- 我今天最清楚的教训是：归因没有完成，判断就不公平。
