<!-- en -->
- I realized a compelling reversal story is still only a candidate; the actual trigger needs its own evidence and rules.
- Turned a trading essay into a reusable playbook with entry conditions, position limits, time stops, and an explicit way to say the thesis failed.
- Added backtests, out-of-sample checks, stress tests, phased rollout, and versioning so the playbook can be challenged before it is trusted.
- Put the finished template where it can be opened from the existing notes workflow, rather than inventing another destination.
- Checked a sector graphic against the underlying assets and caught a category label that could have been mistaken for an entirely different kind of exposure.
- Kept the investment decision view anchored to its frozen morning snapshot instead of quietly mixing in later candidate states.
- Reduced the evening reflection to one evidence-backed sentence; when the source cannot be trusted, a short honest status is better than a polished reconstruction.

<!-- zh -->
- 我今天意识到，反转故事再动人，也只是候选；真正的触发必须有自己的证据和规则。
- 把一篇交易文章整理成可复用模板，写明入场条件、仓位边界、时间止损，以及怎样判定逻辑失效。
- 补上回测、样本外检验、压力测试、分阶段上线和版本管理，让这套方法先接受质疑，再争取信任。
- 成品放进现有笔记入口，不为一份模板再造一个新去处。
- 对照底层资产核查赛道图，发现一个分类标签很容易被误认成性质完全不同的资产暴露。
- 每日投资界面仍以早晨冻结的快照为准，不让后来的候选状态悄悄混进当天基线。
- 晚间复盘收成有证据的一句话；来源不可信时，诚实的短状态胜过圆滑的补写。
