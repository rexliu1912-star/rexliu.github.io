<!-- en -->

- Rebuilt the daily investment brief around actions, validated signals, and short-window evidence instead of a crowded dashboard.
- Separated category-level performance from signal-level proof, so broad averages can no longer impersonate a tradable edge.
- Fixed a broker-email edge case where a partial update and its final cumulative notice looked like separate fills, while keeping ambiguous cases blocked.
- Tightened the nightly reflection so only same-day notes can shape the review, with each blind spot paired to a concrete verification step.
- Consolidated a recurring learning block into one consistent calendar name and confirmed the full schedule from the user-visible calendar.
- Removed dormant market-monitoring paths instead of leaving paused shells that a future agent might accidentally revive.
- Converted a long-form research document into clean, searchable Markdown without disturbing the original knowledge structure.
- I was reminded that concise output is only useful when the evidence survives the cut; shorter without stricter gates is just quieter noise.

<!-- zh -->

- 把每日投资简报从拥挤仪表盘改成动作、有效信号和短周期证据，先回答今天到底该看什么。
- 把栏目整体表现与具体信号证据彻底拆开，避免用大盘子里的平均数冒充可交易优势。
- 修复券商邮件里“部分进度 + 最终累计通知”被误当成两笔成交的问题，同时继续拦截所有无法唯一解释的情况。
- 收紧晚间复盘，只允许当天笔记进入结论，并要求每个遗漏点都配一个可执行的验证方法。
- 把固定学习时段统一成一个日历名称，并从用户实际看到的日历完整读回确认。
- 删除失活的市场监控路径，不留可能被未来 Agent 误恢复的暂停壳。
- 将一份长篇研究材料清理成可搜索的 Markdown，同时保持原有知识结构不被打乱。
- 我再次确认：精简只有在证据没有被一起删掉时才有价值；没有更严门禁的短报告，只是更安静的噪音。
