<!-- en -->
- Expanded the film and media shelf with a fresh batch of titles, local artwork, and a complete build-to-live verification pass.
- Archived two long-form conversations as searchable research notes instead of letting useful ideas disappear into a feed.
- Tightened the way I evaluate pre-IPO hardware stories: orders, customer concentration, and future supply all matter more than the headline narrative.
- Extended the AI infrastructure map beyond chips, power, and financing to the skilled people who actually build and commission facilities.
- Kept industry evidence separate from entry discipline; a stronger demand story still does not justify averaging down without market confirmation.
- I was reminded that a digital garden becomes valuable at the last mile—when an idea is not only saved, but also findable, rendered correctly, and checked in public.

<!-- zh -->
- 给书影页补了一批新作品与本地海报，并把构建、部署到线上验收完整跑通。
- 把两场长对话归档成可搜索的研究笔记，不让有价值的判断消失在信息流里。
- 收紧了对未上市硬件公司的评估：订单兑现、客户集中和未来供给压力，比热闹的行业叙事更重要。
- AI 基建地图不再只看芯片、电力和融资，也开始看真正负责建设与交付设施的技术工种。
- 继续把产业证据和入场纪律分开：需求故事变强，也不能替代资金确认，更不能成为无条件补仓的理由。
- 我再次意识到，数字花园真正产生价值是在最后一公里——内容不只要保存，还要能找到、能正确呈现、能在线验收。
