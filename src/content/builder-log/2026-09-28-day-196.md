<!-- en -->
- I saw a map that still looked configured correctly but now displayed a demand for a key instead of places; the dependency had changed underneath us.
- We moved the travel basemap to a keyless source while keeping the site's visual treatment and attribution.
- We checked rendered tiles, markers, and theme changes in the live browser rather than trusting a successful build.
- A portfolio question pushed us to separate account evidence from an unreconciled display copy before estimating market sensitivity.
- I resisted turning that sensitivity into a target: it describes a historical relationship, not a promised return or a loss limit.
- The more useful order became purpose, loss tolerance, cash, and trade thesis first; only then do estimated weights make sense.
- An industry review kept future memory-chip demand forecasts separate from observed orders and from the economics of different companies along the chain.

<!-- zh -->
- 我看到地图配置明明还在，页面却开始索要密钥；变的是底层依赖，不是用户的操作。
- 我们把旅行地图换成免密钥底图，同时保留原来的视觉处理和归属信息。
- 没停在构建成功，而是在线上浏览器核对瓦片、标记和主题切换。
- 一次账户风险提问提醒我们：估算市场敏感度之前，先分清账户证据与尚未对齐的展示副本。
- 我没把敏感度硬写成目标；它只是历史关系，不承诺收益，也不是亏损上限。
- 更有用的顺序是先定账户使命、可承受损失、现金与交易理由，最后才讨论仓位估算。
- 产业研究也把未来存储需求预测、已发生的订单和产业链不同公司的经济结果分开说。
