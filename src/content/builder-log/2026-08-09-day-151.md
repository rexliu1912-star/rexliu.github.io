<!-- en -->

- Built a beta-chain radar that watches connected companies across AI compute, consumer devices, gold, crypto, and strategic materials.
- Replaced broad oversold alerts with five state changes: reversal, first volume expansion, bottom launch, extreme oversold, and overheating.
- Routed the radar into the morning briefing instead of creating another standalone notification stream.
- Tightened weekly knowledge discovery to a maximum of three final candidates, so research depth is not diluted by a long list.
- Added a freshness gate before deep research: candidates that already moved sharply are marked and deprioritized rather than presented as untouched opportunities.
- Repaired historical portfolio-data inconsistencies and made a full integrity readback mandatory after future trade writes.
- Fed real weekly content performance back into the writing system, including the uncomfortable fact that one recurring series still carries most of the account.
- I was reminded that the smartest system is often the one that adds signal without adding another place to look.

<!-- zh -->

- 搭了一套贝塔链雷达，把 AI 算力、消费电子、黄金、加密与战略资源里的关联公司放进同一张观察网。
- 不再只看宽泛的超卖，而是拆成五种状态变化：拐点、首日放量、底部启动、极端超卖与过热。
- 雷达不再单独制造一条通知流，而是安静供数给每天本来就会看的早报。
- 每周知识扩展最终只保留最多三个候选，避免长名单把研究深度摊薄。
- 在深研之前先加时效门禁：已经快速上涨的候选会被明确标记并降级，不再包装成尚未启动的机会。
- 修复了历史组合数据的不一致，并把交易写入后的完整读回变成强制步骤。
- 把真实的周度内容表现反哺进写作系统，也正视一个固定系列仍然扛着大部分账号表现这件事。
- 我今天再次确认：真正聪明的系统，往往是增加了信号，却没有增加一个必须查看的新地方。
