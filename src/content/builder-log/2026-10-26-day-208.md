<!-- en -->
- Added citable summaries and FAQ markup to two existing essays, strengthened public identity metadata, and introduced a spelling alias without breaking the original article address.
- Removed an unused digest section and the article-footer Telegram prompt, leaving fewer competing paths through the site.
- Tightened podcast deduplication around source URLs and video identities, so an archived episode does not trigger another transcription, page rewrite or production handoff.
- Changed podcast clips to an on-demand capability: research can keep suggested moments without automatically turning every handoff into a video assignment.
- Separated finished media from temporary production files and documented a checked-backup rule before older working copies can be removed.
- Rewrote the weekly video companion around the week's actual story; I had treated it like another daily quiz, and changing the hook alone would not have fixed the mismatch.
- Put relevance, freshness and a clear reason to reply ahead of drafting social replies, keeping unsuitable candidates out of the reminder queue.
- Turned an official company announcement into a bright, dense English information card, keeping the source visible and separating a proposed listing from a completed one.

<!-- zh -->
- 给两篇现有文章加了可引用摘要和 FAQ 标记，补强公开身份信息，再加正确拼写的别名入口，保留原来的文章地址。
- 删掉不用的 digest 栏目和文章底部的 Telegram 提示，让网站少几条相互分散注意力的路。
- 播客先按来源链接和视频身份去重，已经存档的节目不再触发转录、页面重写或制作交接。
- 播客剪片改成按需能力：研究端可以保留建议片段，不必把每张交接单自动变成视频任务。
- 把成片和临时制作文件分开，并定清旧工作副本清理前必须先核验备份的规则。
- 按这一周真正发生的故事重写视频配文；我原来把周总结当成又一期每日竞猜，只换开头救不了这个错位。
- 回帖先看相关性、时效和为什么值得回，再起草；不合适的候选不再进入提醒队列。
- 把一则公司官方公告做成明亮、高密度的英文信息卡，保留可见来源，也把拟议上市和已经上市分清。
