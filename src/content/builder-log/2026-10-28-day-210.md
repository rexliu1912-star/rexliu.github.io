<!-- en -->
- Turned a full education lecture into a bright Chinese information card, keeping the closing questions in the companion summary instead of stopping at the neat framework.
- Published an article about turning a personal reading habit into a daily SNEK video; I wanted to show the work behind the format, not pretend it appeared fully formed.
- Took the warm-paper report presentation into a narrated landscape video, with larger text and a simpler visual reading order.
- Wrote the daily video companion around newcomers and a changing community leaderboard, leaving the repeated guessing-game hook behind.
- Shortened the evening report's input path: start with a prepared team handoff, add later changes, and keep a full-source fallback when that handoff is missing.
- Closed the writing day with separate records for published examples, actual edits, and abandoned directions, so the next draft has something more useful than another generic style prompt.

<!-- zh -->
- 把完整教育讲座做成明亮的中文信息卡，结尾问答仍保留在配套摘要里，没有摘完漂亮框架就停下。
- 发布了从个人阅读习惯做到每日 SNEK 短片的文章；我想讲清这个形式怎么一点点做出来，而不是装作它一开始就完整存在。
- 把暖纸报告展示做成带旁白的横版短片，字更大，视觉阅读顺序也更简单。
- 每日视频配文改从新加入的成员和榜单变化切入，不再沿用已经重复的竞猜开头。
- 缩短晚报取材路径：先读整理好的团队交接，再补后续变化，交接缺失时才回退完整来源。
- 收工前把已发样例、真实改稿和放弃方向分开记录，让下一稿能读到具体反馈，而不是再多一段通用风格提示。
