<!-- en -->

- Changed the real-time alert pipeline so its cursor advances only after semantic processing succeeds.
- Added focused checks for timeout, irrelevant, normal, and no-topic paths before restarting the service.
- Traced a missed macro quote to upstream source coverage instead of blaming the downstream classifier.
- Kept the speed tool narrow: no extra analysis layer, no reflexive source expansion, and no new moving parts without evidence.
- Reviewed recent company calls and separated stronger business evidence from prices that still did not justify chasing.
- Cleaned stale IPO states so already-listed companies stop consuming daily attention.
- I was reminded that a fast polling interval is just a timer; the cursor is the actual contract that decides whether an event survives.

<!-- zh -->

- 调整实时提醒管线：只有语义处理成功后才推进游标，失败则留在原位重试。
- 在重启服务前，分别验证了超时、无关、正常和无主题四条路径。
- 把一条漏掉的宏观发言定位为上游来源覆盖问题，而不是误怪下游分类器。
- 继续守住速度工具的边界：不加分析层，不因一次漏报盲目扩源，没有证据就不增加新部件。
- 复核近期公司电话会，把基本面增强与“价格仍不值得追”分开判断。
- 清理过期 IPO 状态，让已经上市的公司退出每日注意力队列。
- 我今天再次确认：轮询快只是定时器快，真正决定消息会不会活下来的，是消费游标。
