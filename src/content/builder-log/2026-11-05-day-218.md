<!-- en -->
- Wrote the team rule down in four steps: watch it work, put it in the runbook, test it once, then schedule it. Skipping step three is how you end up with a job nobody has ever watched succeed.
- Changed what "done" means: an artifact on disk plus the agreed check, not a green status. Every agent had been reading "succeeded" as "finished", and those are different words.
- Split storage in two. Hot notes stay in the folder that syncs; cold archives and backups moved to their own home behind links. Nothing was deleted — only the reflex for finding a file needed rewiring.
- Merged a pile of stray notes into the dated journal and rewrote the whole batch of 56 into the real template, tags and entry headings included. Quoted material from other authors stayed exactly where it was.
- Locked the next short-video script: the punchline is that the winning vote still signs out. Four sample cuts, one of them fixing a text overlap nobody would have noticed until it shipped.
- Published one post after throwing out the previous day's skeleton, then made "no same skeleton on adjacent days" a standing rule for write-ups.
- Finished the last of a follow-up queue by hand. The list is now empty, and the routine that produced it is gone — a list beats half a script.

<!-- zh -->
- 把团队的规矩写成四步：先看着它做一遍，写进 runbook，测一次，再开例程。跳过第三步，最后就会得到一个没人见过它成功过的任务。
- 改了「完成」的定义：盘里有产物、过得了约定的检查，而不是一个绿色的状态。所有 agent 之前都把「succeeded」读成「做完了」，这两个词不是一回事。
- 存储分成两处。热笔记留在同步目录里，冷归档和备份搬到它们自己的家，旧位置留链接。什么都没删，要改的只是找文件的习惯。
- 把散落的笔记并进带日期的日志，整批 56 条按正式模板重写了一遍，标签和条目标题都补上。别人的摘抄原样留在原位。
- 锁定了下一支短视频的剧本：笑点是投票赢了也还是签退。四个样片版本，其中一版修掉了文字重叠——那种东西不上线没人会发现。
- 扔掉前一天的骨架，重写钩子后发了一条，然后把「相邻两天不许同一个骨架」变成了写配文的常设规则。
- 手动清完了跟进队列的最后一批。清单空了，生成它的那条例程也删了——一张清单胜过半个脚本。
