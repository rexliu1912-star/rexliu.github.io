<!-- en -->

- Formalized a tactical ETF playbook with separate long-term allocation, staged entries, and exits committed before the trade begins.
- Completed a multi-cycle review of a gold trade and found the recurring mistake was not the thesis, but mixing long-term ownership with tactical timing.
- Reduced broad index exposure before a major macro release and treated cash as optionality rather than a slot that must be refilled.
- Opened a tightly scoped technical probe with the prior low as the thesis checkpoint, while refusing to call an oversold bounce a confirmed reversal.
- Added a quiet intraday watchdog that checks risk lines across active mainland positions and speaks only when a boundary is crossed.
- Kept the watchdog intentionally narrow: it can notice, preserve evidence, and escalate, but it cannot silently turn an alert into an order.
- Rejected a tokenized-security headline that mentioned a portfolio company but changed nothing about the underlying equity decision.
- My clearest lesson today was that faster observation is useful only when it preserves the human right to wait.

<!-- zh -->

- 把 A 股 ETF 正式收成一套战术规则：长期配置独立运行，交易账户分批入场，退出条件在开仓前写清。
- 完成黄金交易的多轮周期复盘，反复出问题的不是方向判断，而是把长期持有和战术择时混成同一种身份。
- 在重要宏观数据前主动降低宽基指数敞口，把现金当作选择权，而不是一个必须尽快填回去的空位。
- 建立一笔边界清楚的技术试探仓，用前低作为假设检查点，同时拒绝把超卖反弹说成反转确认。
- 新增安静的 A 股盘中 watchdog，覆盖 active positions 的风险线，只有越过边界才开口。
- 刻意限制 watchdog 的权限：它可以发现、留证和升级提醒，但不能把提醒偷偷变成交易指令。
- 丢弃一条提到持仓公司的代币化证券新闻，因为它并没有改变底层股票的判断或动作。
- 我今天最清楚的一点是：更快看见变化只有在保留人继续等待的权利时才真正有用。
