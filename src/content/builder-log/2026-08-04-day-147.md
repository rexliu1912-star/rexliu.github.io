<!-- en -->

- Rebuilt the political news radar around a single fast loop: detect the relevant update, classify only critical events, and deliver immediately.
- Removed regime labels, market lookups, and multi-layer recommendations because every extra step was slowing down a tool whose main job is speed.
- Kept only a short direction-level deduplication window, which cuts repeated alerts without asking the system to invent more structure than it needs.
- Turned a missed data-center trade-policy headline into a source-quality benchmark: lead time, coverage, and operating cost now matter more than feature count.
- Upgraded the agent runtime and protected the latest user turns from aggressive context compression during long conversations.
- Separated responsibilities more clearly: the radar finds and delivers facts; the research stack and the human terminal handle interpretation.
- My first instinct was to make the radar smarter. The better move was to make it smaller—and therefore faster, clearer, and easier to trust.

<!-- zh -->

- 把政治新闻雷达重新收敛成一条快速链路：发现相关更新，只分类 critical 事件，然后立刻送达。
- 删除 regime、市场查询和多层推荐，因为每多一步，都在拖慢一个本该只负责速度的工具。
- 只保留 direction 级短窗口去重，减少重复提醒，但不强迫系统凭空制造更多结构。
- 把一条漏掉的数据中心贸易政策消息变成信息源验收标尺：首发领先时间、覆盖率和运行成本，比功能数量更重要。
- 升级 Agent runtime，并保护长对话里最近的用户消息不被过度压缩。
- 重新划清职责：雷达负责发现和送达事实，投研系统与人的终端负责解释和判断。
- 我的第一反应是让雷达更聪明；真正更好的动作，是把它变小——于是更快、更清楚，也更容易信任。
