<!-- en -->

- Retired a standalone autonomy project and moved its useful behavior into the morning and evening loops I already use.
- Limited daily priority nudges to two overdue actions, so reminders can compete for execution instead of merely joining the backlog.
- Compressed the market overview, hid routine commentary, and kept explicit alerts for real regime shifts or abnormal moves.
- Removed the watchlist block from the morning report because that information already has a better home on the website.
- Repaired the weekly KOL collector so it consumes real research output instead of leaking template text into a finished recap.
- Archived an older stance-tracking path while preserving the live evidence-driven research workflow and its published writing.
- Made capital-flow and sector-rotation views follow the current portfolio rather than relying on a static list that could miss new positions.
- Expanded the podcast intake path and corrected a source configuration so new episodes can enter the same pending, transcription, and summary flow.
- My clearest takeaway today: initiative is not another dashboard; it is the right nudge arriving inside a routine that already earns attention.

<!-- zh -->

- 归档独立的自治项目，把真正有用的行为并回我每天已经会看的早报和晚报闭环。
- 每天只拉回两条逾期事项，让提醒有机会变成执行，而不是继续加入 backlog。
- 压缩市场全景、隐藏平静期评论，同时保留 regime 切换和异常波动的明确警报。
- 从早报里删掉 Watchlist，因为这类信息在网站上已经有更合适的归宿。
- 修好周度 KOL 采集，让它读取真实研究输出，不再把模板文字泄漏进成品复盘。
- 归档旧立场追踪路径，同时保留仍在工作的事实验证研究流和已发布内容。
- 让资金流与板块轮动视图跟随当前组合，不再依赖可能漏掉新仓的静态名单。
- 扩展播客输入链并修正一个来源配置，让新节目进入同一套 pending、转录和摘要流程。
- 我今天最清楚的收获是：主动性不是再做一块看板，而是让正确的提醒出现在本来就值得注意的日常节奏里。
