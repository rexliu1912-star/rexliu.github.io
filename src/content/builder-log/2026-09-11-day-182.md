<!-- en -->

- Upgraded brokerage reconciliation so a screenshot-first trade becomes the canonical broker record when final evidence arrives, instead of creating a duplicate.
- Added constrained matching across instrument, direction, size, trading date, and provisional price evidence; ambiguity still stops the write.
- Replayed every affected ledger atomically after reconciliation and verified that positions and cost bases stayed coherent.
- Bound legacy transactions to canonical order identities without changing the underlying position quantities.
- Made portfolio publishing fail closed: incomplete source reads now preserve the last safe snapshot instead of emitting a plausible partial portfolio.
- Closed a Chinese equity position through the full lifecycle, including its plan, monitoring, news coverage, and public display state.
- Compared mainland-accessible energy funds by economic exposure, separating integrated-energy beta from higher-volatility upstream production beta.
- I was reminded that the best automation does not remove every pause: facts should flow automatically, while reflection stays manual enough to slow decisions down.

<!-- zh -->

- 升级券商对账：截图先入账的交易，等最终证据到达后直接升级成 canonical broker 记录，不再重复新增。
- 把标的、方向、数量、交易日和占位价格证据收进受限匹配；只要有歧义，写入仍然立即停止。
- 对账后按受影响标的原子重放完整账本，并验证仓位与成本口径没有漂移。
- 为历史交易绑定权威订单身份，同时保持底层持仓数量不变。
- 网站发布改为 fail closed：任一关键来源读取不完整，就保留上一份安全快照，不再生成看似合理的残缺组合。
- 一笔 A 股仓位完成全生命周期收口，计划、监控、新闻覆盖与公开展示状态同步清理。
- 比较大陆可买能源基金时回到经济暴露本身，明确区分综合能源 beta 与波动更高的上游生产 beta。
- 我再次确认，最好的自动化不是消灭所有停顿：事实可以自动流转，复盘仍要保留足够的人工摩擦，让决策慢下来。
