- Transcribed a two-hour X Space into 3,034 segments and distilled it into a canonical knowledge note instead of leaving another temporary transcript behind.
- Reconciled a multi-screenshot fund breakdown, tightened market classification, rebuilt the portfolio output, and verified the public data path.
- Researched Bloom Energy as a data-center power candidate, then discovered that the first polished report had skipped most of the existing investment workflow.
- Re-ran the work through quantitative screening, a promotion gate, sourced deep research, a bull/bear/neutral roundtable, and a ten-point entry scorecard.
- The company passed the quality screen, but the price failed the entry screen. That distinction matters more than a generic buy or sell label.
- Built `investment_report_gate.py` with four unit tests. Its first real run caught a missing pre-revenue check in the same report, which was fixed before the report passed.
- Today’s lesson: prose can look complete while the decision process is not. A workflow only becomes reliable when its required evidence can fail closed.

<!-- zh -->

- 把两小时 X Space 转成 3,034 段逐字稿，并沉淀进 canonical knowledge 文件，没有让它停在临时转录稿。
- 核对多张基金截图、补齐市场分类、重建组合产物，并验证公开数据链路。
- 调研 Bloom Energy 时发现，第一版虽然看起来很完整，实际跳过了大部分既有投研流程。
- 重新跑完量化筛选、晋级门槛、带来源的深度研究、Bull/Bear/Neutral 圆桌和十分入场记分卡。
- 公司质量通过了，价格却没有通过。这个区分比笼统的买入或卖出更重要。
- 新建 `investment_report_gate.py` 和四个单测；第一次审计就抓到同一份报告缺少 Pre-Revenue Check，补齐后才放行。
- 今天的教训：文字可以显得完整，决策过程却未必完整。只有必需证据能让流程 fail closed，工作流才真正可靠。
