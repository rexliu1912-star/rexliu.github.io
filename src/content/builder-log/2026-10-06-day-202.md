<!-- en -->
- Narrowed recurring investment research to Bitcoin, gold, and US indexes instead of looking for the next stock.
- Paused company-event research, catalyst extraction, supply-chain research, and new-stock discovery while keeping their archives.
- Kept the morning portfolio workflow focused on prices, macro facts, and the website rather than company ratings or watchlist scores.
- Kept weekly and monthly reviews tied to real assets, cash flows, and execution, without turning them into another stock-research feed.
- Stopped copying macro events into the personal calendar while keeping market information available for the morning brief.
- Paused scheduled brokerage reconciliation without deleting the scripts or rewriting the trade history.
- Mapped official fund-app and direct-sales routes, separating product availability from permission to subscribe right now.
- I realized that information can be worth knowing without earning a place in my calendar; fewer running tasks can be a deliberate choice, not a missing feature.

<!-- zh -->
- 定期投资研究收窄到比特币、黄金和美股指数，不再不断寻找下一只股票。
- 暂停公司事件、催化剂、产业链研究和新标的发现，历史档案继续保留。
- 晨间组合流程保留价格、宏观事实和网站更新，不再生产公司评级或观察池评分。
- 周报和月报继续核对真实资产、资金流与执行，不把它们变成另一个个股研究入口。
- 停止把宏观事件复制进个人日历，早报仍然可以使用这些市场信息。
- 暂停券商自动对账的定时运行，但不删除脚本，也不改写历史成交。
- 核对基金官方App与直销入口，把“有这个产品”和“现在能申购”分开。
- 我发现，值得知道的信息不一定值得占据我的日历；少运行几个任务，可以是主动选择，不是功能缺失。
