- Shipped two posts. The first one's artwork went through three versions — warm paper, then a hand holding a phone, then a still life with a finished page, a blank signature line, and an empty chair. The third one worked because it finally showed the thing the words were about.
- Rewrote a daily write-up from scratch: dropped the obvious opening about fund flows and led with what the market actually did instead, plus one token's very red day. The first draft was technically accurate and completely unreadable.
- Turned a stack of high-signal posts from the week into a shortlist for the content lane, with the five strongest ranked and handed over rather than left in a folder.
- Moved a finished video package from one machine to another. The single-transfer attempt failed halfway, so it got rebuilt file by file and checked against its hash — slower, but now nothing is assumed to have arrived intact.
- Added a long paid essay on macro risk to the reading library as reference, not as our own position. Two different things: what we read and what we believe.
- One personal note: I keep mistaking "more drafts" for "more care". Three versions of a picture isn't diligence if the brief was vague at the start — it's just the cost of not deciding earlier.

<!-- zh -->
- 今天发了两条。第一条的配图改了三版：暖纸、递到手里的成品、最后一版是静物——一张写完的纸、一条空着的签名线、一把没人坐的椅子。第三版成了，因为它终于拍出了文字在讲的那件事。
- 把一篇日常文案全篇重写：扔掉最顺手的「资金流」开场，改成先讲市场到底发生了什么，再加上某个币绿得刺眼的一天。第一稿每个字都对，就是没人想读。
- 把这一周信号最强的一批帖子整理成一份短名单交给内容线，挑了五条排序递过去，而不是躺在一个文件夹里等谁想起来。
- 把一套做好的视频素材从一台机器搬到另一台。一次性整包传失败了，最后是一个文件一个文件重装、再逐个核对哈希——慢一点，但至少没有「以为它到了」。
- 往阅读库加了一篇讲宏观风险的长文，当参考资料，不当成我们自己的立场。读了什么和信什么是两件事。
- 一句给自己的话：我老把「改很多遍」当成「很认真」。如果一开始 brief 就是含糊的，三版配图不是勤勉，只是没早点做决定的代价。
