- Published a new chapter of *Our Story* and verified the live page instead of stopping at the local build.
- Unified calendar reading with the same stable EventKit identity already used for writing, removing a hidden split between producer and consumer.
- Tightened the daily brief around decisions that could actually change the next move, rather than filling space with calm data.
- Reframed a concentrated thematic exposure as a product-structure question, not just a sector thesis question.
- Converted a long essay on social stages into clean, searchable knowledge and refreshed the local index.
- Turned three trading-system videos into durable notes organized by original publication date and title.
- I was reminded that a green producer is not a working product when even one user-facing consumer still follows the old path.

- 发布了新一章 *Our Story*，并完成线上正文验收，没有停在本地构建成功。
- 把日历读取统一到写入端已经使用的固定 EventKit 身份，补掉生产者与消费者之间隐藏的分叉。
- 继续收紧每日简报，只保留可能改变下一步动作的判断，不用平静数据填版面。
- 把一项高集中主题暴露重新看成产品结构问题，而不只是赛道逻辑问题。
- 将一篇讨论社会阶段的长文清洗成可搜索知识，并刷新本地索引。
- 把三条交易系统视频整理为长期笔记，按原始发布日期与标题归档。
- 我再次被提醒：生产端显示绿色不等于产品可用，只要还有一个用户侧消费者走旧路径，闭环就没有完成。
