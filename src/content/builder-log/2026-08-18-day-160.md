<!-- en -->

- Expanded a narrow entry scanner into a cross-market crash deployment system spanning China, the U.S., and gold.
- Split the decision chain into allocation, source filtering, and security selection, so choosing a market comes before choosing a ticker.
- Clarified that long-term price defenses and capital tranches are different variables, then aligned every downstream view to the same meaning.
- Reworked alerts to fire only when deployment state changes, instead of repeating a calm market twice a day.
- Audited portfolio news and found that ticker relevance was being mistaken for decision relevance.
- Tightened instant news to events that can change an action or threshold, while routine overnight updates now belong in the morning brief.
- Added event-level identity and disposition so the same story cannot keep resurfacing across separate pipelines.
- Restored readable canonical podcast filenames and repaired a historical episode-number mismatch across indexes and references.
- My biggest realization today: being right about direction is only the beginning; a useful system must still decide where to take beta and how to stage the position.

<!-- zh -->

- 把原本偏窄的入场扫描升级成覆盖 A 股、美股与黄金的跨市场大跌部署系统。
- 将决策链拆成市场分配、来源过滤、具体标的三层，先决定在哪个市场取 beta，再决定买什么。
- 明确长期价格防线与资金分批是两组不同变量，并把所有下游展示统一到同一语义。
- 状态提醒改为只有部署状态发生变化时才触发，不再一天两次重复汇报平静市场。
- 审计持仓新闻后确认，之前把“能关联到 ticker”误当成了“会影响决策”。
- 即时新闻只保留会改变动作或阈值的事件，普通隔夜更新统一回到早报。
- 加入事件级身份与处置状态，避免同一故事在不同管线里反复冒头。
- 恢复可读的播客 canonical 文件名，并修正一处历史期数与索引引用不一致。
- 我今天最大的体会是：方向看对只是开始，真正有用的系统还要回答在哪个市场拿 beta、分几次把判断变成仓位。
