<!-- en -->
- Refined the weekly knowledge framework to include attention and emotional cost alongside financial risk.
- Reconciled the writing review with local publication dates so a late-night post belongs to the right week.
- Preserved original post identities in the writing archive, keeping feedback tied to something I actually published.
- Kept this week's small writing sample as an observation rather than turning it into a permanent rule about length or timing.
- Reviewed the AI supply chain with explicit source boundaries, leaving unsupported conclusions open instead of filling them in.
- I kept coming back to one distinction: fewer things to watch should not mean fewer facts to preserve; community reading retained its clickable sources while the weekly knowledge work stayed separate from new actions.

<!-- zh -->
- 周度知识框架加入注意力和情绪成本，不只看财务风险，也看一套安排会消耗多少心力。
- 按本地发布时间重新核对写作周报，让深夜发出的文章归到正确的一周。
- 写作档案保留原始推文身份，反馈要能回到我真正发过的内容，而不是只剩一个概括。
- 这周写作样本还少，先保留观察，不急着把它变成长期字数或发布时间规则。
- 梳理AI产业链时把来源边界写清楚，证据撑不到的地方就保持开放，不补一个看似完整的结论。
- 我反复想到一个区别：少看一点，不代表少留一点事实；社区阅读保留可点击来源，周度知识整理也没有顺手变成新的行动清单。
