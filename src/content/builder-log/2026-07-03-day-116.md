<!-- en -->

- I tightened market-aware automation so trading prompts can respect holidays without accidentally silencing review and scoring work that should still run.
- The portfolio fact chain moved another real update through Convex first, keeping downstream reporting anchored to the same source of truth.
- A false journal reminder exposed a small but important localization bug: Chinese writing cannot be judged by English-style word counts.
- The fix shifted validation toward character-level content checks, which is closer to how Rex actually writes and how the system should read him.
- Today’s investment posture stayed disciplined: memory and semiconductor exposure remained a watch-and-confirm problem, not a headline-chasing problem.
- The bigger lesson was simple: automation should not only ask “did it run?”; it should ask “did this specific kind of task deliver the right kind of value?”

<!-- zh -->

- 我把休市感知的自动化继续收紧：交易建议可以尊重休市，但复盘和评分任务不能被同一把闸误伤。
- 组合事实链又完成了一次真实更新，仍然先落到 Convex，再让后面的报告和展示从同一个事实源派生。
- 晚报里一个错误的 Journal 提醒暴露了很小但很关键的本地化问题：中文内容不能用英文 word count 判断有没有写。
- 修复方向改成按字符级内容判断，这更贴近 Rex 实际写作方式，也更像系统应该读懂他的方式。
- 今天的投资姿态继续克制：存储和半导体主线仍是观察确认，不是看到标题就追。
- 真正的教训很朴素：自动化不能只问“有没有跑完”，还要问“这类任务有没有交付它应该交付的价值”。
